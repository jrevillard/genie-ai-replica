## Waxing

This refers to applying a thin layer of edible wax to the fruit, either through a bath or spraying, followed by drying.

This has the following advantages:

- Improves appearance (the fruit looks shiny)
- Reduces water loss (prevents wilting)
- Extends shelf life

<!-- image -->

## Storage

To accumulate fruit for a large shipment or for a later delivery date, mangoes can be stored for 2-3 weeks in a refrigerated room at 12°C and with high humidity. Lower temperatures can cause damage, especially if the fruit is still green. The cost of storage should be added to the total cost, so it is only worthwhile to store fruit if it can be sold at a higher price later.

<!-- image -->

## Artificial Maturation

Mangoes that have been cut and properly ripened can be ripened with ethylene gas or a product that generates it (e.g., ethephone). This has the advantage that the entire batch ripens uniformly, and develops the optimal aroma and taste expected at the point of sale. To apply ethylene, the mangoes are placed in a sealed room or covered with plastic. The recommended temperature is 24°C, for 24 hours with the ethephone, then ventilated, and the mangoes will ripen in the following days.

## Introduction:

The term "post-harvest" refers to everything that happens after the harvest, regarding the quality of the fruit, handling, and the care required to minimize losses and meet consumer expectations. Many factors affect post-harvest quality, so it is important to consider aspects of both pre- and post-harvest.

## Ripening

To achieve the best results, it is important to remember that mangoes belong to the climacteric fruit group. Their ripening is characterized by an increase in the fruit's respiration rate and significant changes in its color, aroma, firmness, and taste. Ripening can be initiated by small amounts of ethylene gas, produced by other ripening fruits or artificially. Mangoes should be harvested before they are fully ripe to avoid damage in the field, to withstand handling and transportation, and to reach their full maturity on the consumer's table. To achieve this, harvesters need to be trained to cut the fruit at the optimal harvest stage. Each variety has its own indicators, including:

- The shape and size of the fruit
- The color and texture of the skin
- The firmness of the pulp when touched

In addition, when opened, you can observe:

- The color and firmness of the pulp
- The sugar, acidity, and dry matter content, and the taste of the pulp

## Harvesting

The following tools or techniques can be used to improve the quality of the work:

- Pruning shears
- Baskets to reach and cut the fruit
- Buckets for carrying the fruit, placing paper at the bottom of the baskets for transport.

It is important to prevent the fruit from coming into contact with the ground and becoming stained with the "milk" or latex that comes from the mango, so the following is recommended:

-  Do not cut too early in the day
-  Cut the mangoes with a piece of wood, and trim one of the holes in the net so that it drains without staining the others
-  Wash the fruit with an appropriate detergent

## Breathing and transpiration

Fruit as it ripens requires oxygen and generates carbon dioxide (CO 2 ) and heat, in addition to its water evaporating, which causes it to lose weight and appearance. To reduce these effects, it is important not to leave the harvested fruit in the sun and to allow ventilation to lower the temperature after harvesting.

## Pre-sorting and washing

Defective or damaged fruits are removed. The fruit is washed in potable water, which also contains chlorine or another disinfectant to destroy the germs that could later affect the quality of the fruit and the health of the consumer.

<!-- image -->

## Sorting and packaging

The fruit is sorted by size if required by the customer. It is placed in crates or containers, which must be clean, and protected during transport. Depending on the case, the fruit is counted or weighed, and its variety, quantity, quality, and origin are also identified, or as required by the customer. If indicated, it is labeled with the brand to create or take advantage of its prestige.
