# Huduma Service Centres

Huduma centres are one-stop physical locations where citizens can access
services from multiple government departments without travelling between
offices. Each centre hosts counters for the most-requested services.

## Appointment Booking

Citizens book a slot through the national portal (see
[Service Directory](./service_directory.md)) and receive a queue reference.
Walk-in visits are accepted when capacity allows, at lower priority than
booked slots.

## Services Offered

- Identity document collection after approval
- Civil registration certificate collection
- Payment of service fees for applications initiated online
- Submission of supporting documents for pending applications

## Escalation

Unresolved counter cases are escalated to the centre supervisor and, where a
departmental decision is required, to the liaison officer of the responsible
ministry ([Ministry of Public Service](./ministry_of_public_service.md)).
