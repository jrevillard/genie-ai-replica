## Sorghum Cultivation

## Optimal conditions for sorghum cultivation

For sorghum cultivation, the soil must be well prepared, loose, and free of weeds. With sufficient moisture, at least after three rains have fallen and the soil has been moistened to a depth of about 10 to 12 centimeters, without waterlogging, to ensure good germination, the ideal planting depth is 2 to 4 centimeters. The preparation work helps to expose pests on the soil surface for their control and greater water infiltration. Before planting, it is recommended to take soil samples for pest analysis, selecting five points in the planting area. In each point, a hole 30x30x30 centimeters (1 foot square) in width, length, and depth is made, and then the soil from each hole is removed and examined. If a "blind" chicken grub is found in each sampling hole, it is recommended to apply control measures to prevent plant losses and replanting.

## Seed quantity and spacing for sorghum cultivation

To achieve success in sorghum production, it is necessary to establish between 160,000 and 170,000 plants per hectare, with planting spacing between rows of 70 to 75 centimeters and between plants of 8 to 10 centimeters.

Before establishing the crop, it is recommended to perform a germination test. If at least 85 plants emerge from every 100 seeds, it is necessary to use between 15 and 20 pounds of seeds per hectare.

If the producer uses their own seeds, it is necessary to select the best plants and stalks from the center of the field, which must be healthy and free of pest and disease damage.

It is recommended to treat the seeds with mycorrhizae to improve root development, nutrient absorption, and protection against fungi.

## Weed control practices

Weeds are one of the factors that limit sorghum production. It is important to keep the field clean for the first 40 days. Failure to do so could result in a loss of up to 80% of the harvest.

The methods for weed control in sorghum are:

- Manual, using tools such as machetes and hoes.
- Mechanical, using equipment such as plows, discs, cultivators, and harrows.
- Chemical, using herbicides, as a last resort. It is recommended to use products with green tape, which are environmentally friendly and safe for human health.

## Soil fertilization

It is necessary for producers to know the availability of nutrients in their soils in order to develop a good fertilization plan for their crops.

There are two important times for sorghum fertilization:

1.  At the time of planting: In the central and eastern parts of the country, it is recommended to fertilize with 2 quintals per hectare of complete fertilizer 12-30-10 and/or 10-30-10.
2.  25 days after the crop emerges: It is recommended to use 2 quintals of urea (46-0-0) per hectare.

To complement the fertilization of the crops, producers should implement agroecological soil management practices such as NO BURNING, incorporating healthy green manure, organic fertilizers, green manure, and crop rotation.

## Practices for pest and disease control

For pest and disease control, it is necessary to prepare the soil well, removing infected weeds, eliminating host weeds in the rows, and planting varieties resistant to pests and diseases.

It is important not to plant very narrow rows to reduce the incidence of pests in the field. This should be done by respecting the recommended planting densities.
