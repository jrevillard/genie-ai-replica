## Fruit borer infestation in avocado cultivation

<!-- image -->

The fruit borer is a pest that can cause significant damage to avocados by piercing young shoots and fruit, causing holes in both young and mature fruit. It also affects side branches during the vegetative and flowering periods.

- It is recommended to conduct a fruit and branch sampling.
- Weed control.
- Balanced nutrition and sufficient watering.
- Collect infested fruit found on the tree and bury them.
- Beauveria bassiana, 144 gr/ 200 lt water.
- Bacillus Thuringiensis 6.4 WG, 15 grs / 4-gallon sprayer.
