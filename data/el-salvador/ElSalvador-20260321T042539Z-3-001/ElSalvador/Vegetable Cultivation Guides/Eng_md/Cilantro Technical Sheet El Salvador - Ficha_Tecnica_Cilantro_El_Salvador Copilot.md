## Technical Data - Cilantro (Coriandrum sativum L.) - El Salvador

## 1) Crop Identification

Common Name: Cilantro | Family: Apiaceae | Cycle: Annual of 50-90 days (leaf), 90-120 days (seed). Height: 30-70 cm. Pivot and branched root system. Use: Fresh leaves and seeds as a spice.

## 2) Climatic Requirements (Optimal Conditions for El Salvador)

| Factor                 | Requirement                                                              |
|------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Temperature            | Optimal 15-22 °C for leaf; >30 °C induces early flowering. Sow in |
| Relative Humidity       | 50-75 %. Avoid excessive foliar humidity to reduce fungal growth.            |
| Light                    | 4-6 hours of direct sunlight; in heat, prefer filtered / partial shade.  |
| Altitude                | Suitable from 14 to 2,800 msnm.                                                 |
| Precipitation per cycle | 300-400 mm; in rainy conditions, use raised beds and good drainage.              |

## 3) Soil Requirements

Loamy to loamy-sandy or loamy-clay soils, loose, deep, fertile, and well-drained. pH 6.0-7.5 (optimal 6.2-7.0). High organic matter (recommended 20-30 t/ha of well-decomposed compost). Avoid waterlogging; prepare raised beds during the rainy season.

## 4) Sowing and Density

Direct sow to a depth of 1-2 cm. Spacing between rows 20-30 cm; between plants 5-10 cm. Seed rate: 15-25 kg/ha (may vary depending on commercial objective and equipment). Germination in 5-10 days.

## 5) Irrigation

Maintain constant moisture without waterlogging. Light and frequent irrigation (especially establishment) or drip irrigation. Avoid nighttime irrigation to reduce foliar diseases.

## 6) Recommended Fertilization (Adjust to Soil Analysis)

| Nutrient        | Recommendation                                                            |
|------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Nitrogen (N)    | 60-100 kg/ha in total; fraction (establishment and growth). Avoid |
| Phosphorus (P2O5)   | 50-80 kg/ha when preparing the soil.                                           |
| Potassium (K2O)    | 60-100 kg/ha; promotes vigor and quality.                                  |
| Organic Matter | 20-30 t/ha of mature compost to improve structure and retention.           |

## 7) Pest and Disease Management (PMP)

Common pests: aphids, thrips, whiteflies. Diseases: leaf spots (Alternaria), mildew, rotting due to excess humidity. Measures: staggered plantings, crop rotation, early weeding, watering in the morning, ventilation and drainage, use of chromatic traps, removal of foci, and authorized biological inputs.

## 8) Harvesting and Post-Harvest

Harvest for leaves at 45-60 days (depending on climate and variety); for seeds at 90-120 days. Harvest in the cool hours; cool quickly; maintain at 0-5 °C and high HR to prolong shelf life.
