## Recommendations for Beekeepers

Good practices should ensure comfortable, feeding, and hygienic conditions within the hives, establishing an appropriate environment for the bee colony. The following actions are recommended for beekeeping:

- Use of appropriate personal protective equipment.
- Monitoring for pests in the beehives.
- Maintaining hives in weed-free areas, avoiding the use of herbicides, and controlling ants and frogs.
- Hives must be placed on individual stands, at least 45 centimeters from the ground, with a minimum distance of 1.5 meters between hives and in rows of three stands, with a minimum separation of 2 meters, which will allow for good management and adequate ventilation.
- Located at least 3 km away from potential sources of contamination, such as landfills, industrial factories, crops treated with agrochemicals or pesticides.
- Water sources must be of good quality, free of residues, metals, and other contaminants. These should preferably be located within 1 km.
- Carry out routine inspections, keeping a record of all activities performed.
