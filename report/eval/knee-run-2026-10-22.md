## 3. What happened (plain words)

- **Coverage:** knee beat both fixed strategies everywhere it matters — 88% of
  passages retrieved, 83% of questions receiving *complete* evidence. It adapts:
  one chunk when one clearly wins, several when the question genuinely spans topics.
- **Answer quality:** faithfulness rose again (0.874 → 0.897 → **0.911**) — more
  evidence made answers *more* grounded, not less. The granite judge consistently
  rewards the broader context.
- **The price:** precision 0.38 / noise 0.62 — knee grabs ~4 chunks where ~1.7
  would do. Every extra chunk costs LLM tokens and, in theory, risks distraction;
  in practice the judge found no such degradation on this corpus.
- **answer_relevancy** slipped slightly with context breadth (0.527 → 0.496) —
  broader grounding makes answers a bit longer/more rounded. The counter-lever is
  the **concision prompt** (N.9d), not the strategy.

## 4. Verdict + recommendation

**knee_threshold is the best cell measured** — highest recall, highest complete-evidence
rate, highest faithfulness, with the adaptive pick-count behavior you wanted
(1 when one suffices; ~5 when the question spans topics; ~2 documents per answer).
Cost: ~2.3× slice-1's context tokens per query and lower precision.

Recommended sequence: **switch production to knee_threshold**, then land the
**concision prompt** and re-measure (expect relevancy ↑, faithfulness held),
and revisit everything after **N.8 re-chunking** (smaller chunks will shrink knee's
pick counts and likely lift precision back up).
