## Appendix: IEEE HT and ITU GenAI for Good Challenge - Specific Provisions

**For GenAI for Good Challenge Participants:**

**A.1 Challenge-Specific Agreement.** By participating in the **GenAI for Good Challenge** (the "Challenge") organized by IEEE Humanitarian Technologies (IEEE HT) and the International Telecommunication Union (ITU), and by submitting Contributions to the GENIE.AI Project as part of this Challenge, You agree to be bound by this CLA. All Challenge submissions are considered Contributions under this agreement.

**A.2 Challenge Overview.** The GenAI for Good Challenge is a global initiative connecting innovators and changemakers to tackle real-world problems in health, agriculture, and climate resilience. The Challenge focuses on three specific use cases, each tied to a UN Sustainable Development Goal (SDG):

1. **Agriculture Extension Chatbot** (Country: Lesotho; SDG 2 - Zero Hunger)
   - A mobile-friendly AI chatbot offering farmers and agricultural advisors in Lesotho real-time advice, crop alerts, and market guidance—via voice, text, and basic phones.

2. **Non-Communicable Diseases (NCD) Prevention Chatbot** (Country: The Gambia; SDG 3 - Good Health and Well-Being)
   - A multilingual AI chatbot delivering evidence-based, personalized guidance to help Gambians prevent and manage chronic health risks like hypertension and tobacco use.

3. **Extreme Weather Advisor** (Country: Bangladesh; SDG 13 - Climate Action)
   - An AI-powered assistant that helps Bangladeshi farmers prepare for droughts with localized weather alerts, water-saving tips, and crop-specific risk guidance.

**A.3 Submission Deadline.** Contributions submitted for the Challenge must comply with the submission deadline of **1 December 2025**. Late submissions may not be considered for evaluation or funding.

**A.4 Funding and Awards.**
- Up to **$25,000 USD in funding** is available for winning teams.
- Submission of a Contribution does not guarantee any award, prize, funding, or recognition.
- The Challenge judges' decisions are final.
- Funding is contingent upon compliance with all Challenge requirements and this CLA.

**A.5 Real-World Deployment Focus.**
- The Challenge is focused on **real-world deployment** of solutions in partnership with UN in-country offices and local organizations.
- Participants must be prepared to collaborate with IEEE HT, ITU, and global partners to develop responsible generative AI solutions with measurable impact.
- Contributions should be designed for practical implementation in the target countries (Lesotho, The Gambia, or Bangladesh).

**A.6 Structured Process.** The Challenge unfolds over several phases:
- **Application Phase**: Initial submission of proposals and team information
- **Evaluation Phase**: Review of submissions by Challenge organizers
- **Prototyping Phase**: Development of working solutions
- **Deployment Phase**: Real-world implementation in target communities

Participants agree to engage with each phase as required by the Challenge organizers.

**A.7 Post-Challenge Rights.** All Contributions submitted during the GenAI for Good Challenge become part of the GENIE.AI Project and are subject to the same terms and conditions as other Contributions, including licensing under Apache 2.0. Winning teams may be required to participate in deployment activities, documentation, and knowledge-sharing sessions.

**A.8 Team Contributions.** If submitting as a team:
- All team members must agree to this CLA.
- The team lead represents and warrants that they have obtained agreement from all team members to be bound by this CLA.
- All team members retain joint responsibility for compliance with the terms of this CLA.

**A.9 Data Handling.** Challenge participants must:
- Use only publicly available or provided datasets for the target use cases.
- Not introduce any sensitive, confidential, or proprietary data without explicit written permission.
- Comply with all applicable privacy laws and regulations in the target countries.
- Properly document all data sources and provenance in accordance with STANDARDS.md.
- Respect local data sovereignty and community rights.

**A.10 Ethical AI and Human-Centered Design.** Participants must:
- Design solutions that are human-centered and respect the needs and priorities of local communities.
- Ensure AI systems are responsible, fair, and do not perpetuate bias or discrimination.
- Consider accessibility, including support for basic phones and low-bandwidth environments where applicable.
- Align with the UN Sustainable Development Goals and the humanitarian mission of IEEE HT and ITU.

**A.11 Collaboration Requirements.** By participating, You agree to:
- Collaborate with UN in-country offices leading implementation efforts.
- Engage with IEEE HT, ITU, and Challenge partners as required.
- Share knowledge and contribute to the broader GENIE.AI community.
- Support the transition of solutions to sustained, locally-led implementation where feasible.
