# Ministry of Public Service and Human Capital Development

The Ministry of Public Service and Human Capital Development (formerly styled
in various combinations involving Public Service, Performance and Delivery
Management, Gender, etc.) is the central government ministry responsible for
public-service policy, human-resource management standards, performance
management, and the coordination of citizen-facing service-delivery platforms
including the Huduma Kenya programme.

Official presence: https://mps.go.ke/

## Mandate (high-level)

Derived from Executive Order organisation of government (notably Executive
Order No. 2 of 2023 and subsequent reorganisations), core functions include:

- Public-sector reforms and transformation, operational standards, and process
  engineering.
- Government human-resource information systems and services.
- Internship and volunteer policy for the public service.
- Government payroll policy and standards.
- Government shared support services.
- Research, development, and public-service delivery innovations.
- Public-service career planning and development.
- Administration of insurance and welfare programmes for the civil service
  (group life, last expense, work-injury benefits, personal accident cover).
- Oversight of the National Youth Service (NYS).
- Counselling policy and services.

Vision (as published): a centre of excellence in performance management with
an agile and responsive workforce for quality service delivery.

Mission (as published): to provide policy leadership, advisory services,
technical support, and coordination of human-resource and performance
management for efficient service delivery in the public service.

- Psychological Counselling and Wellness Services

## Agencies under / linked to the Ministry

- **Huduma Kenya Service Delivery Programme** (Huduma Kenya Secretariat) —
  the one-stop-shop platform for physical and multi-channel citizen services.
  See [Huduma Service Centres](huduma-kenya.md). Official site:
  https://www.hudumakenya.go.ke/
- National Youth Service (NYS)
- Kenya School of Government (KSG) — capacity building for public officials

## Relationship to service delivery

The Ministry sets workforce policy and service-delivery standards that apply
across government departments. It owns correspondence-tracking processes used
between departments and the escalation path for service-centre cases that
require a departmental or policy decision.

The master list of services and the practical “how to obtain them” guidance for
citizens live in the [Service Directory](service-directory.md). Day-to-day
digital transactions are handled by eCitizen (see
[eCitizen Digital Services & Payments](ecitizen-digital-payments.md)); physical
one-stop delivery is handled by Huduma Centres.
