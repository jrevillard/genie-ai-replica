## Harvesting and Post-Harvest Handling

It is recommended to harvest the plants when they reach physiological maturity. If there is a lot of rain, it is necessary to dry them under cover or in a shed, placing the bunches of plants with the roots facing up on wires supported on posts at a height of 1 to 1.5 meters above the ground.

During the harvest season, the plants are harvested in bunches and placed in rows on the ground with the roots facing up, and exposed to the sun for four to five days before threshing or winnowing.

Once the beans have been threshed and winnowed, they should be placed in the sun to reduce the moisture content to 13%. Subsequently, they should be placed in bags for immediate sale or stored in metal silos or another structure that ensures the preservation of the grain's quality. It is important to apply a treatment to prevent the occurrence of storage pests.

## CENTA Drought-Tolerant Bean Variety

This is a red bean variety, developed by CENTA in collaboration with the International Center for Tropical Agriculture (CIAT). It has high yield potential, tolerance to the main diseases affecting the crop, and good grain color for commercialization. Its main attribute is tolerance to moderate drought and high temperatures.

This variety is a sustainable alternative to reduce the effects of climate change on bean cultivation.

## Recommendations for Management

In prepared soils: Perform two passes with a harrow, the first to incorporate the straw, to a depth of 30 cm, and the second (crossing the land) before planting to homogenize the soil.

On slopes: Straw should be spread or dragged across the soil surface, and weeds should be removed manually or with chemical products (green herbicide).

Seed: It is recommended to use improved seed produced by CENTA or by authorized producer associations.

Planting density: The recommended amount of seed for apples is 80 pounds, with a planting spacing of 50 centimeters between rows, placing two to three seeds per spaced planting at a distance of 20 centimeters.

Fertilization: It is recommended to perform soil analysis before planting. Generally, it is suggested to apply 2 to 3 tons of formula 18-46-0 at planting and 1 ton of 46% urea 25 to 30 days after planting.

## Disease Control

For yellow mosaic, common mosaic, downy mildew, angular spot, and Roya, in the presence of normal insect vectors or conditions that allow these diseases to affect the crop, it is recommended to use high-quality seed from improved bean varieties generated by CENTA; and to perform two preventative applications of broad-spectrum systemic fungicides 22 and 35 days after planting. During the flowering period, planting should begin before December 15, and applications of a specific insecticide for whitefly control should be made 15 to 20 days after planting.

## Weed Control

Manual weeding is recommended 15 to 20 days and 25 to 30 days after planting, or by applying pre-emergence and post-emergence herbicides that are tolerant of the crop.

## Pest Control

For foliar pests: Whitefly, leafhopper, tortoise beetle, thrips, leaf miner, pod beetle, and stem borers, in areas with high incidence, it is recommended to use seed treatments and to perform three applications of specific organic or chemical insecticides, 15, 22, and 35 days after planting, as a preventative measure. If the applications are made with organic products, the application times should be reduced.

## Seed Production

To produce seed of this variety, a systematic process of selecting plants with good characteristics and health should be carried out; starting before the beginning of flowering and continuing until harvest and grain processing. To maintain the characteristics of the variety, it is important to renew the seed every two or three years.
