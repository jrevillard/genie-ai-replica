Huanglongbing (HLB), also known as citrus greening or yellow dragon disease. The symptoms typically appear primarily on the leaves, but also on the fruit. Infected trees exhibit areas with yellowish or pale green shoots, and multiple flowers blooming out of season, which mostly fall off.

## Symptoms of HLB in citrus crops

- Yellow or pale shoots
- Diffuse yellowing or mottling
- Green islands, thick and corky veins
- Yellow veins
- Thickening of the central vein - hardened veins
- Excessive leaf drop
- Productive death of the plant
- Excessive fruit drop
- Small and deformed fruits, with a thicker and paler skin, which remains green on the lower part.
