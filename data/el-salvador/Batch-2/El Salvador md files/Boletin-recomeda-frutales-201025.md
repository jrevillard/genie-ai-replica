## Recommendations for Avocado Harvesting

- Do not apply any type of systemic pesticides one month before harvest.
- Disinfect all tools and equipment to be used during harvesting.
- Do not hit or injure the fruit.
- Separate the harvested fruit from the ground, damaged fruit, and fruit picked from the tree.
- Use ladders and sharp pruning shears or other implements to harvest, avoiding dropping the fruit.
- Handle the fruit carefully when loading them into plastic bins.
- Use 70% of the capacity of the plastic bin and use clean material at the bottom to prevent damage from impact and excessive weight.
- Harvest in the early morning to avoid stress and dehydration.
