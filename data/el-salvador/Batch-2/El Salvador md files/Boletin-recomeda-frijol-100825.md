## Recommendations for Planting Winter Beans

<!-- image -->

- This involves planting beans in an area where corn has reached its physiological maturity, interspersing the beans between the corn rows. The beans are planted on both sides of the corn row, spaced 20 to 25 cm from the row; placing 2 seeds per hill with a spacing of 20 cm between plants.
- It is important to select a well-drained area that does not have excessive or insufficient moisture, as this can affect plant development.
- Weeds between rows should be controlled manually or chemically.
- If the land is sloped, planting should be done along the contour lines to reduce soil loss and nutrient runoff.
- Conduct a germination test before planting. Before planting, treat the seeds with an insecticide and fungicide to prevent pest and soilborne disease attacks.
