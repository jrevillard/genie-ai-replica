=== Post-Install Steps ===
1. Verify GPU node pool is ready:
   kubectl get nodes -l gpu-pool=true

2. Verify NVIDIA Device Plugin:
   kubectl get daemonset nvidia-device-plugin -n kube-system

3. Check all pods are running:
   kubectl get pods -n {{ .Values.namespace }}

4. Verify Ingress has external IP:
   kubectl get ingress -n {{ .Values.namespace }}

5. Test health endpoint:
   kubectl port-forward svc/health-monitor 8080:8080 -n {{ .Values.namespace }}
   curl http://localhost:8080/health-status
