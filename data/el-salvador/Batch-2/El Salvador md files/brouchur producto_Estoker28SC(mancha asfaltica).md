## AGRICULTURAL USE:

Agricultural fungicide.

## MODE AND ACTION:

ESTOKER ® 28 SC is a fungicide composed of Azoxystrobin, which has a systemic and acropetal action with slow activity and translaminar activity, but with uniform distribution throughout the leaf, in combination with cyprocona-zole with translocation via xylem and contact; having preventive, curative and eradicating activity.

Azoxystrobin inhibits the electron flow of the mitochondrial respiration of fungal cells, interfering with their life cycle, primarily in the processes of spore germination, infection and hyphal growth. Cyproconazole inhibits the biosynthesis of ergosterol, which is an essential component of the fungal cell wall membrane.

## APPLICATION EQUIPMENT:

ESTOKER ® 28 SC can be applied with ground equipment. Foliar application with a manual back-siphon pump, at a constant pressure of 45 psi with a special TX-12 nozzle for applying fungicides. Before using the equipment, it should be checked to ensure that it is in good working order, without leaks and with the correct calibration.

## MIXING PREPARATION METHODS:

Fill the sprayer tank with water to half, add the correct dose of the product, then complete the required volume of water. Shake well to obtain a homogeneous mixture before starting the application. The recommended pH for mixing is 4.5 - 5.5. After application, thoroughly clean the application equipment. Always ensure you have auxiliary equipment such as buckets, brushes, brushes, dosers and other tools that will help you in the preparation and application of this product. Personnel who handle this product should use personal protective equipment: rubber boots, mask, goggles, rubber gloves, doser and application equipment.
