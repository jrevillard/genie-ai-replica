Lesotho Crop Variety Recommendations

RECOMMENDED MAIZE VARIETIES FOR LESOTHO:
PAN 3M-01: High yielding maize variety recommended for Maluti highlands and Lesotho lowlands. Drought tolerant and disease resistant.
PAN 12: Early maturing maize variety for lowlands and Maluti highlands. Drought tolerant, disease resistant, good taste when roasted, performs well in poor soils.
PAN 4M-19: High yielding maize variety, excellent yield when irrigated. Resistant to leaf spot and stalk rot diseases.

RECOMMENDED BEAN VARIETIES FOR LESOTHO:
Pinto NW 590: Higher yielding than traditional Lesotho bean varieties. Disease resistant.
Pinto Olathe: Higher yielding than Pinto NW 590. Disease resistant. Suitable for many areas of Lesotho.
Kranskop Red Speckled Sugar Bean: Very high yielding. Large seeds. Does not split. Disease resistant.
PAN 148 Red Speckled Sugar Bean: Resistant to bean mosaic and BCMV diseases. Grows in many areas of Lesotho.
PAN 178: Good yield. Disease resistant.

PLANTING SEASONS IN LESOTHO:
Maize: Plant October to December during rainy season.
Beans: Plant October to November during rainy season.
Highlands: Maluti mountains and foothills.
Lowlands: Mabalane and lower elevation areas.

Source: Lesotho Ministry of Agriculture - LESELI LA TEMO extension materials.
