## I. INTRODUCTION

Modern agriculture has long demanded the use of chemical technologies in crop management, but the indiscriminate use by small, medium, and even large-scale producers has led to a series of problems, such as: chronic health problems for producers, soil and groundwater contamination, and loss of biodiversity.

In vegetable cultivation, due to the high presence of pests that affect them, producers need to seek alternatives to traditional chemical products, which are so harmful to both the environment and beneficial insects.

For many years, producers have exclusively relied on the use of conventional agricultural technologies, and there is currently a demand for methodologies and technologies with a more environmentally friendly approach, such as ecological insecticides, which are easy to prepare, are efficient, and very low cost.

## II. BORDELAIS SOLUTION

Bordelaise Solution repels mites and prevents diseases such as anthracnose and mildew that affect most horticultural crops.

## What is Bordelaise Solution?

It is a solution whose ingredients are copper sulfate, hydrated lime, and water. It has been used for centuries to treat plant diseases, as it acts as an excellent fungicide and acaricide. Its name comes from the French Bouillie Bordelaise, who invented it with the aim of treating diseases in grapes (wine).

## Uses

- Bordeaux mixture is used for the "prevention" of diseases caused by fungi such as anthracnose and blight in stems, leaves, and fruits in vegetable, fruit, and ornamental crops; in the nursery stage, it prevents stunting.
- It is a contact fungicide that forms a surface film, which prevents the fungus from penetrating plant tissues and prevents the pathogen from developing.
- As a Bordeaux paste, it is used to cover lesions on trees damaged by gummosis, and as a pruning sealant for fruit trees and vegetables.

Bordeaux mixture "does not have a curative effect," it only prevents the disease from developing in other parts of the plants.

## Application method

It is applied as a spray, on both trees and herbaceous plants, to prevent the appearance of the diseases mentioned above or to stop their spread. The application should be made shortly after preparing the product (Figure 6); "a maximum of one day after."

Figure 6. Finished Bordeaux mixture

## When to apply

Apply every seven to eight days, on seedlings in "nursery" conditions. Use 50% solution and 50% water; for plantings in "the field," use 100% of the product (pure mixture).

## Advantages

- Low cost
- Easy preparation
- Ingredients that are easy to prepare (dilute)
- Easy use for the producer

## Limitations

- Must be used on the day it is prepared
- Cannot be mixed with other products
- Can clog the pump nozzle if not prepared properly; it is necessary to remove the sprayer to prevent precipitation.

## III. ORGANIC INSECTICIDE ACHIHOL

This is a product based on garlic, chili, and alcohol, hence its name, which is easy to prepare and uses readily available and inexpensive ingredients that acts as a contact insecticide. It is an organic product that effectively controls aphids, whiteflies, and lepidopteran larvae; and repels the pepper weevil and pepper mite.

## Materials needed to make one liter

- One head of garlic
- Four ounces of chili peppers
- One liter of 90% alcohol or one liter of pure brandy or spirits

## Ingredients:

- One 1-liter plastic container
- One knife

## Procedure

1. Chop or mash the garlic cloves and chili peppers together
2. Place in the plastic container
3. Add 1 liter of 90% alcohol and mix thoroughly, then close the container
4. Let it sit for eight days
5. Strain and package in plastic containers, noting the date of preparation
6. Store in a cool, dry place, out of reach of children

## Dosage to use

The dosage to apply after eight days is 100 cc of the product for a 16-liter backpack sprayer. It is recommended to apply this insecticide every eight days, preferably in the early morning when pests are present in the crop.

## IV. ORGANIC AND BIO-STIMULANT REPELLENT EM-5

EM-5 is a liquid with pest-repelling, fungicidal, and bio-stimulant properties that helps with plant development. It was developed and applied empirically by farmers from different countries, and it has been gradually improved. In the eastern part of El Salvador, EM-5 has been used, with positive results such as improved production, reduced pests and diseases in vegetables and other crops; in addition, it reduces damage to soil, water, and the health of producers.

## Materials for preparing 180 liters of EM-5

- 5 gallons of liquid mountain microorganisms
- 1 gallon of vinegar
- 1 gallon of molasses
- 5 pounds of ginger
- 3 pounds of garlic and 3 pounds of red onion
- 5 pounds of hot chili peppers

- 2 pounds of each of the following ingredients: lemon grass, dead flower, chichicaste, epasina, good herb, eucalyptus, and mint.

In addition to the plants mentioned, any medicinal plant or weed that is observed to be free of pest or disease damage can be used.

## Preparation method

- Place 100 liters of chlorine-free water in the barrel.
- Add 1 gallon of molasses and dissolve it well in the water.
- Finely chop: ginger, onion, garlic, hot chili peppers, and aromatic leaves.
- Add 1 gallon of vinegar and 5 gallons of liquid microorganisms.
- Fill the barrel with water to approximately 180 liters.
- Mix all the ingredients using a paddle.
- Close the barrel.

## Maintenance

- Stir the mixture twice a day for 15 days.
- When the product is ready to be used, it will have a shiny appearance, approximately two to three weeks after preparation.
- Strain the mixture before use to prevent clogging of the sprayer nozzles.

## Storage

It can be stored for up to one year, although it is recommended to prepare only what is needed for the crop cycle.

## Recommendations

- Protect the barrel from sunlight and rain.
- Keep the barrel closed.
- It should be prepared in advance for preventative application in crops.
- Use aromatic herbs and other materials available on the farm or in the community, as indicated in this document.

## Dosage and application

- Apply 200 to 300 cc to the foliage or at the base of the plants, every week.

## Effects

- Primarily repels: aphids, whiteflies, mites, scale insects, caterpillars, corn earworms, and slugs.
- Preventative fungicide.
- Stimulates the vigor and growth of crops.
- To counteract damage caused by diseases, pests, or environmental factors.

## Advantages

- Facilitates the production and marketing of food without agrochemicals.
- Promotes a new culture of production, protecting the environment and the health of producers.
- Does not harm beneficial organisms.

## V. ORGANIC CHIFOR INSECTICIDE

CHIFOR is a botanical insecticide that eliminates all types of insects, such as aphids and scale insects, and also controls whiteflies and mealybugs. It is made from chili peppers, onions, and dead nettle flowers, hence its name.

## Materials to use for making 3 liters

- 3 pounds of chili peppers
- 3 large onions, preferably red
- 3 pounds of dead nettle (entire plant)
- 3 liters of chlorine-free water

## Equipment

- A knife
- A 5-gallon plastic container

## Procedure

- Finely chop and grind the dead nettle plant, chili peppers, and onions.
- Place the processed materials in the container.
- Add the three liters of water and mix thoroughly, then seal the container tightly.
- Let it rest in the shade for 15 days.
- Strain and package in chlorine-free plastic containers, noting the date of preparation.
- Store in a cool, dry place, out of reach of children.

## Dosage to use

- Up to 500 cc of Chifor can be used per 16-liter pump, for the pests mentioned above.
- It is recommended to apply every 15 days, preferably in the early morning.

## VI. ORGANIC CHILAZO INSECTICIDE

This is a botanical insecticide that controls scale insects, mealybugs, aphids, whiteflies, and other pests.

## Materials to use

- 5 heads of garlic
- 5 purple onions
- 50 chili peppers
- A strainer
- 1 gallon of chlorine-free water

## Procedure

- Mash the garlic, chili peppers, and slice the onions.
- Pour the water into a container and bring to a boil.
- Add the materials, and let it simmer for 10 minutes, then cool and strain the product.

## Dosage to use

- It is recommended to use 150 cc per 16-liter pump, for the pests mentioned above, applying preventatively every 8 days, preferably in the early morning.
