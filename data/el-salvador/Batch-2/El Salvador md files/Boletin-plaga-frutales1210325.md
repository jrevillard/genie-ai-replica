## What is Fusarium Race 4 Tropical (R4T)?

<!-- image -->

R4T is a pathogenic fungus transmitted through the soil, which attacks the roots of the banana or plantain plant and causes the disease known as wilt, by blocking its vascular system. This strain is one of the most aggressive fungi that has ever existed.

Currently, the most effective approach to combating R4T is to prevent its spread to clean areas as soon as it is detected.

Prevention and phytosanitary measures are the most effective ways to control the spread of the fungus.

Diversity is also crucial for preventing the spread of R4T. Diversifying crops and making better use of available genetic resources are key to promoting long-term resilience. R4T arrived in Latin America in 2019.

R4T cannot be cured, only prevented. Once in the soil, there are no effective treatments, whether chemical or otherwise. Fungicides for the soil are not effective in controlling or eradicating R4T from infested soils.
