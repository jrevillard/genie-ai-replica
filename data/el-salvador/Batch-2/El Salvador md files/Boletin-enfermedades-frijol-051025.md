## Fungal diseases affecting bean cultivation in El Salvador

<!-- image -->

These diseases are favored by rainy weather, moderate to high temperatures, and high relative humidity. They appear on the leaves, stems, branches, and pods of the bean plant, and at any stage of development. They are transmitted through crop residue, infected seeds, and diseased plants from neighboring fields. The diseases are spread by rain and wind.

For prevention and management, it is recommended to use certified seeds, remove crop residue from the previous harvest, rotate crops, and use ground cover (rice husk, sugarcane or corn leaves, or dead weeds) to reduce the effects of splashing caused by rain. In moderate attacks, the use of systemic fungicides is recommended, and in severe attacks, chemical control is recommended.
