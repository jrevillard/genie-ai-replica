## Who administers the scheme

The forestry subsidy scheme is administered by the Natural Resources
Directorate. The directorate publishes the conditions each January and
decides every application within ninety days of submission.

## Conditions of the forestry subsidy

The conditions of the forestry subsidy are four. First, the parcel must hold
at least three hectares of sustained-yield forest, verified by the
directorate's field survey. Second, the applicant must file a management plan
certified by a registered forester before the first payment. Third, payments
are capped at one hundred and twenty euros per hectare per year and continue
for five years while the survey stays positive. Fourth, clear-cutting the
subsidized parcel during the commitment period repays every installment with
interest.

Applications for the forestry subsidy open on the first working day of March
and close when the yearly budget line is exhausted.
