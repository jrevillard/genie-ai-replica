## B. CHARACTERISTICS

Priori Xtra is a systemic fungicide that combines the action of azoxistrobin and ciproconazole, which belong to the chemical groups of Strobilurins and Triazoles, respectively. It should be applied as a foliar spray to control diseases in the crops listed below.

The combination of these active ingredients allows for control at different stages of pathogen development, such as spore germination, penetration, and mycelial growth.

The best results are obtained when Priori Xtra is applied after the first symptoms of the disease have been detected and before the disease has become fully established.

## G. PHYTOXICITY

Priori Xtra, when applied at the recommended dose, does not cause any phytotoxic effects on the crops recommended here.

## H. INCOMPATIBILITY

For mixtures of products with Priori Xtra, it is recommended to use products that have current registration in the crops indicated here. The product can be mixed with other pesticides whose formulation is granular, wettable powders and emulsifiable concentrates.

## 'RESISTANCE MANAGEMENT'

'TO PREVENT THE DEVELOPMENT OF POPULATIONS RESISTANT, ALWAYS RESPECT THE DOSAGE AND THE FREQUENCIES OF APPLICATION; AVOID THE REPEATED USE OF THIS PRODUCT, ALTERING IT WITH OTHER CHEMICAL GROUPS WITH DIFFERENT MODES OF ACTION AND DIFFERENT MECHANISMS OF DETOXIFICATION AND THROUGH THE SUPPORT OF OTHER CONTROL METHODS'.

## I. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION MEASURES

- 'WHILE HANDLING THE PRODUCT, AVOID CONTAMINATING SOILS, RIVERS, LAKES, STREAMS, RESERVOIRS, CANALS, OR WATER RESERVES. DO NOT WASH OR DISPOSE OF PESTICIDE RESIDUES OR EMPTY CONTAINERS IN THEM.'
- 'HANDLE EMPTY CONTAINERS AND PRODUCT RESIDUES IN ACCORDANCE WITH the General Law for the Prevention and Integrated Management of Waste, its regulations, or the Pesticide Empty Container Management Plan, registered with SEMARNAT.'
- 'INAPPROPRIATE USE OF THIS PRODUCT CAN CONTAMINATE GROUNDWATER. AVOID HANDLING IT NEAR WATER SOURCES AND DO NOT APPLY IT WHERE THE WATER TABLE IS SHALLOW (75 CM DEEP) AND THE SOIL IS VERY PERMEABLE (SANDY).'
- 'IN CASE OF SPILLS, PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT SHOULD BE USED, AND THE SPILLED PRODUCT SHOULD BE RECOVERED USING SOME ABSORBENT MATERIAL (FOR EXAMPLE, SOIL OR DIRT). COLLECT THE WASTE IN A SEALED CONTAINER AND TAKE IT TO THE CLOSEST AUTHORIZED HAZARDOUS WASTE COLLECTION CENTER.'
- 'PERFORM A THREE-WASH OF THE EMPTY CONTAINER AND DISPOSE OF THE RINSE WATER IN THE RESERVOIR OR CONTAINER WHERE THE MIX FOR APPLICATION IS PREPARED.'

'THIS PRODUCT IS EXTREMELY TOXIC TO ANIMALS (FISH, AQUATIC INVERTEBRATES) AND AQUATIC PLANTS (ALGAE AND VASCULAR PLANTS)'
