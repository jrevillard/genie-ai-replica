## SORGO CENTA S-2 bmr: A New Forage Variety

National Center for Agricultural, Livestock and Forestry Technology "Enrique Álvarez Córdova Dd" (CENTA)

December 2011

<!-- image -->

## ORIGIN

The CENTA S-2 bmr sorghum is the result of six years of genetic improvement work, carried out through a collaboration between CENTA and INTSORMIL. The "bmr-12" gene was incorporated into the traditional CENTA S-2 variety. In 2004, eight "B" lines with the "bmr-12" gene were received from Purdue University, USA.

The same year, at the Experimental Station of Santa Cruz Porrillo, crosses were initiated between the received lines and the commercial CENTA S-2 variety. This variety was used as the female parent, and the bmr lines as the male parent. A single backcross was sufficient to recover all the favorable characteristics of the traditional CENTA S-2.

The selection process was carried out using the "Genealogical Selection" methodology. After six selection cycles at the San Andrés Experimental Station, in 2008, the new CENTA S-2 variety with the fully incorporated and stable "bmr" gene was obtained.

## IV. GENERAL RECOMMENDATIONS

If the purpose is to produce forage, CENTA S-2 bmr can be sown in May, August, or during the dry season with sufficient moisture or irrigation.

A good practice is to sow in May-June to harvest the forage in August, and the second harvest can be for grain or re-cut for hay; when harvested for grain, the straw can be dried and chopped for use as hay during the dry season; conversely, if the purpose is solely to obtain grain, it should be sown in August to harvest it during the dry season.

CENTA S-2 bmr is adaptable from 0 to 1,500 meters above sea level, in sandy, loamy, and well-drained soils.

## 1. Land Preparation:

The soil should be well-loosened and free of weeds, especially in non-sugarcane fields. It is important that the sorghum germinates and develops in weed-free land.

## 2. Spacing:

A spacing of 70 centimeters between rows is recommended in cultivated and uncultivated land (when sowing with cuttings, stakes, or ridges).

## 3. Seed Treatment:

It is recommended to do this before sowing with products for controlling soil pests such as stalk borer and cutworm, applying Imidacloprid 70 WS at a rate of 48 g per 20 lbs of seed per manzana (approximately 1 hectare).

## 4. Sowing:

Plant between 8 and 10 plants per linear meter, as planting too densely increases competition for water and nutrients and makes the plants more susceptible to lodging due to low lignin content. To achieve this planting density, use 20 lbs of seed per manzana (approximately 1 hectare), which would provide a population of 140,000 plants per manzana.

## 5. Thinning:

This is an expensive practice that can be avoided if the recommended planting density is maintained. However, if more than 10 plants per linear meter are present, thinning should be carried out 15 days after emergence, as this is when the actual population is established. When the crop is left with a high population, the plant does not express its full genetic potential, reducing the volume and quality of the forage for ensiling.

## 6. Fertilization:

Before implementing any fertilization plan, it is recommended to have a soil analysis and fertilize based on the results. However, in general, it is recommended to apply 5 days after emergence, 4 qq/mz (approximately 4 tons per hectare) of a 16-20-0 fertilizer, and a second application 30 days after sowing, with 4 qq/mz (approximately 4 tons per hectare) of ammonium sulfate. If the purpose is to obtain grain, a third application 40 days after sowing with urea is recommended, applying 150 lbs per manzana.

## 7. Harvesting:

If the goal is to obtain forage, the plant should be harvested when the grain is in the milky-doughy stage, or when it reaches physiological maturity if for grain.

## Second Harvest:

After harvesting the forage, the plants should be cut 10 centimeters above the ground and the field should be thoroughly cleaned. 10 days after cutting, when 90% of the buds have emerged, fertilize with ammonium sulfate at a rate of 4 qq/mz (approximately 4 tons per hectare); the second harvest takes 10 days less than the first, but during the development of the crop, in this phase, the same care should be given as in the first.

## VI. RECOMMENDATION

CENTA S-2 bmr can be used as seed in future plantings, provided that the variety's purity is maintained. To achieve this, it is recommended to remove any off-type plants (plants that are not the normal type) before flowering and before harvest. During harvesting, harvesting, and seed cleaning operations, avoid mixing with other unsorted grains.
