## Bean Rust Disease in Bean Cultivation

<!-- image -->

Bean rust is a disease that causes wilting at the base of the stem and root, with small, elongated, reddish-brown, sunken, and dark spots. The plant can be affected during the first four weeks. The fungus can survive in remnants of previous harvests, so the damage increases when beans are grown in the same area for several years.

## Recommendations:

- -Remove crop residue.
- -Use healthy, new seeds.
- -Plant above raised beds.
- -Avoid waterlogged soils.
- -Do not plant deeper than 3 centimeters in contaminated soils.
- -Treat seeds with fungicides.
