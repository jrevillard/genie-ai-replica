## Measures to prevent diseases and pests in bees

<!-- image -->

Prevention involves a series of activities that beekeepers must implement to prevent the entry, development, and spread of diseases and pests that affect both bee larvae and adult bees in the hives. Key preventive actions include:

- Avoiding the exchange of honeycombs between sick and healthy hives.
- Replacing old honeycombs with new ones made with stamped wax.
- Preparing the feed with potable water, ensuring its safety.
- Keeping the smoker clean by removing residues and attachments resulting from combustion.
- Washing protective equipment after each inspection, avoiding the use of detergents.
- Obtaining certified and disease-free queen bees or nuclei.

The beekeeper should inspect their apiaries every 8 to 15 days to detect signs suggestive of pests or diseases, as well as changes in bee behavior. This information should be included in the apiary's activity and production record.
