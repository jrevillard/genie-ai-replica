# Huduma Kenya

Launched in November 2013 as a Kenya Vision 2030 Flagship Project and formalized via Kenya Gazette Notice No. 2177 of 4th April 2014, Huduma Kenya is an Integrated Service Delivery (ISD) initiative aimed at eliminating the need for citizens to travel between multiple government offices. It is coordinated by the Huduma Kenya Secretariat under the State Department for Public Service, Ministry of Public Service, Human Capital Development and Special Programmes (Executive Order 2 of 2023).

## Service Delivery Channels

* **Huduma Centres:** Approximately 59–61 physical one-stop-shop centres across all 47 counties (Nairobi has multiple: GPO, City Square, Eastleigh, Makadara, Kibra). About 20 centres operate extended hours (typically 7:00 AM–7:00 PM). Staff seconded from various MDAs receive unified Huduma customer service training.
* **Huduma Contact and Tele-Counselling Centre:** Dial `1919` for inquiries, complaints, and updates. Contact centre hours broadly 7:00 AM–9:00 PM weekdays and extended weekends; Tele-Counselling 8:00 AM–5:00 PM weekdays. Also WhatsApp 0747 191919 and info@hudumakenya.go.ke.
* **Huduma Electronic \& Mobile:** Online portal (www.hudumakenya.go.ke) and USSD \*191# for digital access.
* **Huduma Mashinani:** Mobile outreach bringing services to grassroots levels.

## Cost Structure

Huduma Kenya does *not* charge for its services directly. Citizens pay the statutory MDA service fee plus any applicable convenience fee via the [eCitizen](./ecitizen_digital_payments.md) gateway. Cyber cafés / assisted services at centres may charge a small regulated auxiliary fee for scanning, printing, and navigation help (independent AGPO-certified operators).

## Core Values ("Huduma DNA")

Courtesy, Innovation, Integrity, Transparency, Passion. Guiding principle: Service Excellence.
