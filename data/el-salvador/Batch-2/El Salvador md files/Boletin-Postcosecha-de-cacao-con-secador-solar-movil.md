## Building the Dryer

The structure consists of 6 iron bars, made of smooth iron wire, joined together with welding, in the following way:

<!-- image -->

After painting the iron, transparent plastic and mesh are placed, starting with the lower pieces, like laying tiles, and without gluing them down. The plastic is first glued to the iron, then the edges are secured with adhesive tape on top of the plastic. The same is done for the roof.

The wood for the frames should be planed and sanded to create smooth surfaces for the frames and wire for securing the mesh. The mesh is then glued and nailed in place, ensuring it is securely fastened, as it needs to support the weight of the product. The frames in the photo are 70 cm long and 47 cm wide, but these measurements can vary.

The drying rack can be made in different ways. The design in the illustration allows it to be disassembled and stored in a small space.

For the base, cut the black plastic to the required size to cover the floor.

## Description of Mobile Solar Dryer with Minimal Investment

The purpose of this type of structure is to dry fermented cocoa quickly and efficiently, while protecting it from rain, dust, insects, or other animals. It can also be used for fermentation.

The materials and tools needed to build it are easily found, even in non-urban areas, and do not require much more than a person with welding skills and basic carpentry skills.

The structure is easy to assemble and disassemble, and can be moved or stored for later use.

The "shed" consists of 4 iron bars (1/2 inch), a door, and a roof, all covered with plastic glued to the iron using shoe glue and adhesive tape. Inside the "shed," a wooden rack is placed to support wooden frames with mesh, in which the product is placed, allowing for maximum ventilation. Black plastic is placed on the floor to isolate moisture, prevent insects from entering, and to capture solar heat. The sides have mesh to allow for ventilation and prevent flies from entering.

## Features of the solar dryer

Width and height: determined by the width of the plastic. In the case of commercial plastic measuring 140 cm, the maximum width is 115 cm and the height is 180 cm. Length: can be adjusted from 1.5 to 4 meters.

Usable area: To allow for placing, removing, and retrieving the product, a "corridor" of 40 cm is left for an average person. With 2 meters in length x 1.15 meters in width, there is 1.5 square meters of drying area.

The structure captures solar energy, which increases the temperature of the product being dried and the air inside, increasing its drying capacity.

It is important to regulate ventilation. At the beginning, more ventilation is required because evaporation is high. At the end of the drying process, less ventilation is needed because higher temperatures are required, and the humidity released by the product is low. In addition, the outdoor climate conditions must be taken into account. Therefore, the windows can be closed when needed.

The 80 pounds of fermented cacao that can fit in the boxes corresponds to 250 to 400 ears of medium size. For 5 to 8 ears per weekly harvest per tree, this implies that a shed of 2 meters by 1.15 meters can be used to dry the weekly harvest of 30 to 80 trees.

Fermentation requires less space, and can be done in wooden boxes or other types of containers, inside the dryer or in a less exposed location to nighttime cooling.

## Advantages of using the dryer, compared to drying in an open patio.

## In dry or enclosed environments:

The shed avoids contamination from dust, domestic animals, flies, ants, and other animals. Inside the shed, the temperature rises and is maintained even on less sunny days.

In humid or vegetated environments: By raising the temperature in the shed, the relative humidity of the air decreases, which increases its drying capacity. In the event of rain, the shed protects the product.

## Care and limitations

Since the drying process requires energy, on days with little sunshine, the drying process is slow. In "stormy" conditions, it is not recommended to dry due to the high ambient humidity.

To expose the entire product to the sun, it is necessary to remove it periodically and place it in a thin layer.
