## Recommendations for Corn Fertilization

<!-- image -->

A fertilization plan for corn cultivation involves providing the necessary nutrients for plant growth and development. Ideally, a soil chemical analysis should be conducted to obtain specific technical recommendations. The fertilizer should be applied in portions and incorporated into the soil, with adequate moisture, to ensure its effectiveness. The following fertilization plan is recommended for corn:

## Fertilization Plan for Corn Cultivation

| First Fertilization                                                                                                                                    | Second Fertilization                                        |
|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| At planting or 8 days after planting.                                                                                                             | 22-30 days after planting.                        |
| Recommended formulas: • Formula 15-15-15, 4 quintals per hectare (qq/ha) (259 kg/ha). • Formula 16-20-0, 4 qq per hectare (259 kg/ha). | Ammonium Sulfate 21% N, 4.4 qq/ha (286 kg/ha). |

Place the fertilizer 5-10 cm away from the plant, preferably incorporated into the soil. If the land is on a slope, place it on the upper side of the plant.
