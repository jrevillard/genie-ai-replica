## Recommendations for Beekeeping Management

Good beekeeping management allows for proper control of bee health, safety, and productivity. It is important to have a list of hives and maintain records of activities for each apiary. Producers should be aware that during periods of nectar scarcity, colonies require more frequent feeding due to their increased vulnerability to diseases and pests. Therefore, the following recommendations are suggested:

- Perform an annual queen bee replacement.
- Apply Varroa treatment at the appropriate time.
- Use alternative or natural treatments to control diseases in brood combs.
- Provide supplemental feeding during periods of nectar scarcity.
- Clean weeds within and around the apiary to prevent diseases.
- Collect unusable wax, plastic, or hive parts found within the apiary.
- Replace old, damaged, or diseased combs.
- Manage guard bees during non-harvest seasons to protect the hives.
- Use potable or chlorinated water for cleaning beekeeping equipment and materials.
