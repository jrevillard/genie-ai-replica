## GUIDE FOR PREPARING SULFOCALCINE SOLUTION

<!-- image -->

In the management of vegetable and fruit crops, pests limit production and increase costs. Fortunately, there are low-cost and easy-to-prepare alternatives: one of these is sulfocalcine solution. This is a natural product that acts as a preventative and control agent for fungi and mites, and is permitted for use in organic agriculture. It is made from:

These minerals, in their separate form, have fungicidal action, a property that has been known for many years. Sulfocalcine solution is a very useful product for preventing and controlling diseases caused by fungi such as mildew, powdery mildew, and botrytis, as well as for controlling mites and trips.

IS SULFOCALCINE SOLUTION? Calcium hydroxide (Ca(OH)2), reacts with elemental sulfur "S" to form "calcium sulfide," which has been used as an insecticide. The active ingredient is calcium sulfide, with the following chemical formula: CaS (According to analysis from the 2011 Agricultural Chemistry Laboratory of CENTA).

To prevent pest and disease damage in crops, in addition to using alternative products such as natural fungicides, it is necessary to consider other elements such as the use of cultural practices that influence the health of the crop, such as: avoiding soil compaction, performing phytosanitary pruning (removal of mature and damaged foliage from a crop), managing the spatial distribution of crops for good light and air penetration, removing diseased plants, collecting damaged fruit, preventing people from entering the plantation; disinfecting footwear with a chlorine solution before entering the field area; especially for vegetables.

MATERIALS AND EQUIPMENT FOR PREPARING 20 LITERS OF SULFOCALCINE SOLUTION

Sulfocalcine solution is prepared from sulfur flowers and quicklime or hydrated lime. The following materials and equipment are needed for its preparation: 2 pounds of quicklime, 2 pounds of sulfur, 20 liters of water, a metal container, a wooden paddle, and preferably a fuel-efficient stove with pieces of wood.

## STEPS FOR PREPARING SULFOCALCINE SOLUTION

- 1 Weigh the sulfur and lime, 2 pounds each
- 2 Place 20 liters of water in a metal container and bring to a boil. When the water is boiling, add the sulfur and then the lime.
- It is recommended that the operator use a handkerchief to cover their mouth and nose, to avoid respiratory problems due to inhalation of dust and fumes.
- 3. Stir constantly with a wooden paddle for approximately one hour over a strong fire. The solution will be ready when, after boiling for

Let it stand until it cools, then pour into plastic or glass containers, preferably dark-colored. It can be stored for more than a year.

## APPLICATION

For diseases caused by fungi in vegetable crops such as: onions, beans, sweet peppers, and tomatoes, apply 0.5 liters of sulfocalcine solution per 18-liter pump. For fruit crops such as lemons, mangoes, coconuts, and papayas, apply 2 liters of solution per 17-liter pump.

For control of trips and mites in chili, tomato, and onion crops, apply 0.75 liters per 18-liter pump. The sediment that remains at the bottom of the container can be used as a pruning sealant after performing pruning on fruit trees.

RECOMMENDATIONS Apply in the early morning or late afternoon. Sulfocalcine solution in sealed containers. Do not use water with soil sediment when applying, as this deactivates the product. Do not apply to cucurbit crops (melons, watermelons, squash, beans, cucumbers, and others in this family). Do not apply to beans when they are flowering.

COSTS For preparing 20 liters:

20 liters of water: $0.40, 2 pounds of quicklime: $1.00, 2 pounds of sulfur: $6.00, wood: $2.00. Total: $9.40. Cost per liter: $0.47

ADVANTAGES It is a natural product, low cost. Permitted for use in organic agriculture.

LIMITATIONS Do not use it on legumes when they are flowering: Does not cause premature aging of plants.
