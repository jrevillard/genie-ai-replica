## Nosemosis: a disease affecting bee digestion

<!-- image -->

Nosemosis is a bee disease caused by microsporidians (fungi) that are acquired through contaminated food and water.

These microorganisms infect the intestines of adult bees, causing symptoms such as diarrhea, weakness, tremors, inability to fly, and ultimately, premature death. The most visible signs of the infection are diarrhea stains within the hive and the presence of dying or dead bees in the brood chamber. In severe cases, the disease can lead to the complete loss of the colony. The spread of spores occurs through the feces of infected adult bees.

## Among the techniques used for its management, the following are recommended:

- Hygiene and preventative plan.
- Placing hives in locations with low humidity and minimal shade.
- Replacing or rotating frames in the brood chamber.
- Managing the space within the hive to avoid excessive humidity.
- Replacing queen bees.
- Disinfecting all materials used each season.
- Conducting at least one annual sampling of bees from the apiary for analysis of pests and diseases in a laboratory.
