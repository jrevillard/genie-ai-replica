# Ministry of Public Service and Human Capital Development

The Ministry of Public Service and Human Capital Development (formerly Ministry of Public Service, Performance and Delivery Management under Executive Order No. 2 of November 2023) is tasked with transforming the Kenyan civil service through performance management, customer service excellence, human capital development, and integrated service delivery (ISD).

## Core Mandates

* **Performance Management:** Enforcing Results-Based Management (RBM) and Performance Contracting across Ministries, Departments, and Agencies (MDAs) to ensure accountability.
* **Service Charters:** Mandating Citizen Service Charters across institutions to define the nature, quality, and timeframe of services citizens should expect.
* **Training and Culture:** Operating customer service excellence and Huduma Service Delivery Standards Training, aligning civil servants with private-sector standards.
* **Program Oversight:** Acts as the parent ministry for the [Huduma Kenya](./huduma_kenya.md) Secretariat (established via Presidential Gazette Notice No. 2177 of 2014; coordinated under the State Department for Public Service per Executive Order 2 of 2023).
* **Human Resource Functions:** Human Resource Management Policy, Human Resource Development, Management Consultancy Services, Human Resource Information System Services (HRIS-Ke), Public Sector Reforms and Transformation, and Psychological Counselling and Wellness Services.

## Leadership (as of 2026)

* Cabinet Secretary: Hon. Geoffrey Kiringa Ruku
* Principal Secretary (State Department for Public Service and Human Capital Development): Dr. Jane Kere Imbunya

## Historical Note

Originated as the Establishment Division in 1947. Expanded in the 1960s for training Kenyan citizens. Multiple reorganizations; most recent renaming to Ministry of Public Service and Human Capital Development following Presidential pronouncement after EO No. 2 of 2023.
