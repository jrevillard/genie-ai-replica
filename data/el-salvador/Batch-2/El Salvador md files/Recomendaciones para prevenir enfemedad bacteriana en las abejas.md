## Recommendations to prevent bacterial diseases in bees

It is important for beekeepers to prevent the development of diseases in their bee colonies by following these recommendations:

- Adjust the number of hives in the apiary.
- Place the hives in well-ventilated, partially shaded, and sloped locations, so that rainwater does not accumulate inside.
- Regulate vertical growth, in accordance with the population of adult bees.
- Replace at least eight frames from the brood chamber annually.
- Do not discard honey, wax, propolis waste from one hive in the apiary.
- Ensure adequate feeding and water supply.
- Disinfect any material removed from a hive (frame, lid, and bottom).
