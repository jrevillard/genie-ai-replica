## Deferred / Open (to resolve in PRD or Architecture)

- Exact retention/TTL policy defaults and per-tenant override mechanism.
- PII redaction strategy for *markdown bodies* (Presidio on ingest vs. field-level masking) — must not corrupt OKF conformance.
- Multi-tenancy model: Keycloak realm-per-agency vs. single-realm + claim/scopes (Architecture decision).
- Whether OKF bundles use a *separate* `graph_name` per tenant or a shared `OKF` graph with tenant-tagged chunks + ACL filters.
- Versioning semantics: bundle-level `okf_version` vs. per-concept versioning for citation pinning.
- gRPC internal transport: adopt or defer (REST-only MVP).
