## Recommendations for fruit trees during the heatwave

<!-- image -->

The heatwave is a period characterized by decreased rainfall and higher temperatures. This doesn't mean it stops raining altogether, but rather that it rains less. This period typically occurs between the second half of July and the first few days of August. During the heatwave, droughts can occur, causing water stress for the crops.

## Recommendations for crops

- If you have irrigation available, irrigate the crops during the cooler parts of the day, before 10 am and after 4 pm.

- Maintain ground cover or mulch on the soil to help retain moisture. Control and do not eliminate weeds.
- Plant green manure crops such as cowpea, mucuna, or others to help retain water.
- Apply fertilizer if there is sufficient moisture, or use foliar applications. Consider using compost, wood chips, or chicken manure.
- Constantly monitor for pests.
- Apply amino acids to the crops to help mitigate the stress caused by drought.
