## Recommendations for Bean Cultivation Fertilization

Organic matter improves soil quality and reduces the amount of fertilizer needed for the crop, making it important to incorporate the residues from previous harvests. When soils are poor or depleted, proper fertilization provides the necessary nutrients for the good growth, development, and productivity of the crop.

CENTA recommends conducting a soil analysis before implementing the crop. If a soil analysis is not possible, the following two basic fertilizations are recommended:

- The first, at planting or no later than 8 days after, incorporating 2 qq/hectare (or 4 qq/hectare) of the 18-46-0 formula, or 15-15-15.
- The second, 30 days after planting, with a bag of Urea (150 lb/hectare).
- It is suggested to supplement fertilization through foliar application with a multimineral metalosate at a dose of 1 liter/hectare during the early stage of flowering and 15 days later.
