## Methods to Accelerate Oxygen Reduction in Bags

When the grain is well-dried and has few insects, oxygen consumption is minimal, allowing any present insects to multiply.

One method is to place a plastic bottle filled with a small amount of moistened grain on top of the grain. This will germinate and consume oxygen within a few days.

Another method is to place lit candles on top of the grain, close the bag, and seal it tightly. The flames consume some of the oxygen before extinguishing.

## Main Applications of Special Bags

- 1. Storage of seeds without refrigeration
- 2. Storage of grains without insecticides
- 3. Temporary protection of crops in emergency situations
- 4. Temporary storage in the field or due to lack of storage facilities
- 5. To ensure airtightness during fumigation with phosphine
- 6. Protection during transport for coffee exports, which is the current use in El Salvador

## Protecting the Bags

The plastic bag should be used inside a sack to prevent the weight of the grain from breaking it. Also, be careful of rodents and other animals that may try to bite the bag, especially when it contains corn or sorghum.

An alternative is to place a very large bag inside a smaller barrel, seal the bag, and then close the barrel, so that nothing can damage the bag. Alternatively, several small bags can be placed inside a barrel.

## GENERAL INFORMATION

The purpose of storing grains in completely sealed containers such as special bags and hermetic silos is to prevent the entry of moisture and reduce the amount of oxygen, which prevents the development of insects and fungi.

Tests conducted at CENTA demonstrated the effect of the type of bag on the development of insects and fungi in corn stored for 4 months at 15% humidity:

| Bag           | Live insects per qq   | Fungal odor   |
|-----------------|-------------------------|-----------------|
| Sack            | 16,000                  | Yes             |
| Standard bag | 10,000                  | Yes             |
| Special bag  | 12                      | No              |

Special bags also help to maintain the seed's germination power.

-  To achieve a good seal, remove the air from the filled bag, twist, tie, fold, and tie again with rubber band, tire, or tape. Close the sack or container.

## EFFECT OF MOISTURE

Drying the grain allows for its preservation, and the use of bags reinforces this effect, as it keeps the grain dry, protecting it from moisture during the rainy season.

If harvested during a rainy season, it is very difficult to reduce the moisture content sufficiently. In these cases, the grain (with a moisture content of 14 to 16%) can be stored in special bags to prevent the development of fungi and insects for a short period, until conditions for further drying exist.

For long-term storage, the grain must be dry (at 13% or less). There are various methods for checking the moisture content of the grain; it is always recommended to check the results to be sure.
