Chart 3 · T10 crater (factor=0.0020)

What NOT to do: offline F1 champion + live deploy without RAGAS validation. factor=0.0020 vs factor=0.0006 (validated).
Avg_sel per config
RAGAS faithfulness

What to read

  • factor=0.0006 (current, validated): avg_sel 9.12, faithfulness 0.655 — LLM sees ~9 grounded chunks per query.
  • factor=0.0020 (T10 crater): avg_sel 3.26 (−64%), faithfulness 0.506 (−16.5pt).
  • Root cause: context_decay_cost = factor × token_count dominates adaptive selection. Higher factor = stricter threshold = fewer chunks kept = less context for LLM.
  • Smoking gun: vars.yml:118-119 notes CONTEXT_DECAY_FACTOR=0.0006 was tuned for our MD chunk_size=2000. Chunker tightened to 1400 without re-calibrating.
  • Lesson: offline F1 ≠ live RAGAS. Always validate live before deploying offline champion.

Three takeaways from the Sept 23 → Oct 01 cycle:
1. BGE query prefix wrapper + re-calibrated reranker (T13a) is the new champion: factor 0.0006→0.0005, thresh -0.25→-1.0. RAGAS faithfulness +4.5%, context_precision +9.9%, context_recall +2.4% vs T11 baseline (only answer_relevancy regresses −4.1%).
2. The wrapper + re-cal are stacking fixes, not replacements. T12 (wrapper alone) regressed RAGAS despite gains in anchor quality — re-cal was required to recover the coverage losses. Always re-calibrate the reranker when changing upstream retrieval behavior.
3. avg_sel jump from 4.10 → 9.12 is 100% chunker-driven, not prompt-driven. T13a adds avg_sel 9.12 → 9.64 (+0.52) — within noise of baseline, no concern.
