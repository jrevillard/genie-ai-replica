These are the factors that have the greatest impact on the health of beehives. A healthy hive should maintain an average temperature of 34°C (degrees Celsius) in the brood chamber. At this temperature, the hive functions optimally, and the brood develops satisfactorily.

<!-- image -->

Overcrowding in the brood chamber often triggers swarming behavior (which is detrimental to the beekeeper). If the temperature is too high and the hive lacks adequate ventilation and air drainage, the amount of space needed for the bees to maintain an optimal body temperature will increase. If the bees cannot ventilate the hive properly, the temperature rises, and the bees will tend to leave in order to improve heat dissipation.

High humidity and poor ventilation can cause bees to abandon the hive.
