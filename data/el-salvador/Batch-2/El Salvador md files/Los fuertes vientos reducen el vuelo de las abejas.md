## Strong winds reduce bee flight

According to the Ministry of Environment and Natural Resources, the sky is expected to remain mostly clear and without rain. In addition, north winds will continue throughout this week, from November 26th to December 1st, with speeds ranging from 10 to 22 km/h, with occasional gusts up to 45 km/h, mainly in the high and mountainous areas of the country.

The effect of strong winds can lead to damage in beekeeping, such as:

- Winds of up to 24 km/h significantly reduce bee activity and between 30-40 km/h, bee flight ceases
- Mechanical damage to crops and plantations
- Falling flowers, leaves, and branches on trees above beehives
