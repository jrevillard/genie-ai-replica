Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 May 2026 · Effective immediately

AMINA Care (“AMINA”, “we”, “us”) is a humanitarian primary-health information assistant developed for low-resource clinical contexts, initially deployed in The Gambia. This policy explains what data we collect when you interact with AMINA — on the web, on WhatsApp, on Facebook Messenger, or on Telegram — how we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and how you can have it deleted.

AMINA is being submitted under the United Nations International Computing Centre (UNICC) GenIA for Good programme. The platform is non-commercial and free to use.

AMINA is not a substitute for emergency care. If you are experiencing a medical emergency — chest pain, heavy bleeding, sudden weakness, breathing difficulty, severe injury, obstetric emergency, suicidal thoughts — stop reading and contact your local emergency services or go to the nearest health facility immediately. AMINA provides general health information and triage guidance; it does not deliver clinical care.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

AMINA Care is operated by the AMINA project team in cooperation with healthcare partners and humanitarian sponsors. For privacy questions, data-access requests, or to delete your data:

  • Email: privacy@amina-design.com
  • Web: amina-design.com
  • Postal address: available on request via the privacy email above.

2. What data we collect

Account data (when you sign up on the web)

  • Name, role (patient, caregiver, admin), invite code, and an account password (hashed; we never see it in plain text).
  • Optional profile information you choose to add (date of birth, village, language preference).
  • For caregivers: caregiver identity verification details required by the operating partner.

Health-related data (only when you provide it)

  • Symptom descriptions, vitals, medication notes, photographs of prescriptions, or other clinical information you share in a chat.
  • Voice notes you record for transcription (if you choose to send audio).
  • Alerts raised by you or your caregiver.

Conversation data

  • Messages you send to AMINA and replies generated by the assistant.
  • Channel of origin (web, WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram) and the messaging platform's internal user identifier (e.g. Facebook Page-Scoped ID, Telegram user ID, WhatsApp phone number).
  • Timestamps and, for moderation purposes, automated risk flags (e.g. self-harm signals, abuse-of-system signals).

Technical data

  • IP address, browser type, and approximate region (used for security & abuse-prevention only).
  • Anonymous usage analytics about which features are used and where errors occur.

We do not collect your contacts list, your photos beyond what you explicitly share, your browsing history, or any data from your device's microphone or camera unless you actively send us audio or images in a chat.

3. How we use your data

We use the data above to:

  • Generate replies and clinical-triage information in response to your messages.
  • Route urgent cases to a caregiver or to local emergency services where you have opted into that flow.
  • Keep a clinical record so that follow-up conversations across multiple days remain coherent (we maintain a session memory).
  • Detect and block abuse, jailbreak attempts, spam, and misuse of the system.
  • Improve safety filters and clinical accuracy — aggregated, de-identified statistics only; we do not use the content of your specific messages to train third-party language models.
  • Comply with our legal obligations under the laws of The Gambia and applicable international humanitarian-data norms.

We do not sell your data. We do not run advertising. AMINA is free to use.

4. Legal basis

Where the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or analogous rules apply to a visitor, we rely on:

  • Consent — you choose to message the bot and to share any health information.
  • Vital interests — protecting your health, particularly for urgent or emergency cases.
  • Legitimate interests — running the service securely and preventing abuse.
  • Public interest in the area of public health — AMINA operates under a humanitarian-health mandate.

5. Channel-specific notes

WhatsApp (via Twilio)

Messages between you and AMINA on WhatsApp travel through Twilio Inc. (United States), which delivers them to our servers and back. Twilio sees the message content for the duration of delivery. Each request from Twilio to our servers is cryptographically signed (HMAC-SHA1) so that no third party can impersonate Twilio. While we are operating on the Twilio WhatsApp Sandbox, you must opt in once by sending join milk-shot to the sandbox number; this also appears in Twilio's records.

Facebook Messenger (via Meta)

Messages on Messenger travel through Meta Platforms, Inc. (United States and Ireland). Meta sees the message content for delivery and supplies us with your Page-Scoped User ID (a stable identifier scoped to our Facebook Page only — it cannot be used to look you up anywhere else on Facebook). Each webhook delivery is signed (HMAC-SHA256 with our App Secret) so that no third party can impersonate Meta. Meta's own privacy policy applies in parallel to ours for the portion of the journey on Meta's servers.

Telegram

Messages on Telegram travel through Telegram Messenger Inc. Telegram sees the message content for delivery and provides us with your Telegram user ID. Each webhook delivery carries a static secret token that proves the request came from Telegram; impostor requests are rejected at the network edge.

Web chat

When you chat on amina-design.com, your messages travel directly from your browser to our servers over TLS (HTTPS). They do not pass through any messaging platform.

8. Your rights and how to exercise them

You have the right to:

  • Access — get a copy of the data we hold on you.
  • Rectify — ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete — ask us to delete your data (see § 9 below).
  • Restrict / object — ask us to stop using your data for non-essential purposes.
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent — stop using AMINA at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing that happened before.
  • Complain — lodge a complaint with the Gambia data-protection authority or, where applicable, your local regulator.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@amina-design.com. We will respond within 30 days. If we need to verify your identity before acting (for security reasons), we will ask for the minimum information required.

9. Data deletion request

Quick path: email privacy@amina-design.com from the address associated with your AMINA account, or include sufficient identifying information (channel + handle) for us to locate your data. Subject line: Delete my data.

What happens when you request deletion:

  1. Within 5 working days we acknowledge the request.
  2. Within 30 days we delete or pseudonymise all data we hold on you, with the following narrow exceptions:
    • Records we are legally required to retain (e.g. mandatory reporting of clinical safety incidents).
    • Audit-trail entries that have already been pseudonymised and that no longer identify you.
    • Backups, which are overwritten on their normal rotation cycle (within 90 days at the latest).
  3. We send you a confirmation that the deletion is complete.

For data held by the messaging platforms themselves (Meta, Twilio, Telegram), you must additionally contact those platforms to delete their copy — we have no ability to delete data from their servers on your behalf.

10. How we protect your data

  • All traffic to and from amina-design.com is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+).
  • Cloudflare provides DDoS protection and a Web Application Firewall in front of every public endpoint.
  • Webhook endpoints from Meta, Twilio, and Telegram are cryptographically authenticated; impostor requests are rejected.
  • Access to clinical and audit data inside AMINA is gated behind role-based access control (patient, caregiver, admin) and JSON Web Tokens.
  • Passwords are stored hashed using bcrypt with per-account salts; we never see them in plain text.
  • Our infrastructure is in a single hardened data centre operated under industry-standard physical and network security.
  • We run regular automated security scans and a continuous abuse-defence layer; security incidents are reviewed and remediated as a matter of priority.

No system is perfectly secure. If you become aware of a security issue, please contact us at security@amina-design.com.

11. Children and minors

AMINA is intended for use by adults and by caregivers acting on behalf of patients of any age. Where a minor uses AMINA directly, we expect a parent, legal guardian, or registered caregiver to supervise that use and to have provided consent on the minor's behalf. If you believe a minor has used AMINA without appropriate consent, please contact privacy@amina-design.com and we will delete the data.

12. International data transfers

AMINA is operated by a team distributed across multiple jurisdictions and uses third-party processors (above) located outside The Gambia, primarily in the United States and the European Union. Where data is transferred internationally, we rely on the processors' standard contractual clauses and on the EU adequacy or equivalent frameworks where applicable. Self-hosted components (transcription, translation, clinical reasoning logs) remain on AMINA's own infrastructure.

13. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy as the product and its regulatory environment evolve. Material changes will be announced at least 14 days in advance via in-product notification and, where we have an email address for you, by email. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
