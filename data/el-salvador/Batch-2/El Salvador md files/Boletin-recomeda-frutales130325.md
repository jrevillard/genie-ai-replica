## Environmental factors that influence banana production

<!-- image -->

## Altitude:

The ideal range is between 20 and 600 meters above sea level (masl). Higher altitudes extend the plant's growth cycle.

## Temperature:

The optimal temperature is between 20 and 30 ºC (Celsius). Temperatures below this range can prevent proper fruit development. High temperatures can also cause sunburn on developing plants.

## Water

This crop requires significant amounts of water for healthy growth, so it is recommended to plant in areas where rainfall fluctuates between 1500 and 2500 mm, distributed throughout the year. Monthly water needs are 150 to 180 mm.

## Relative Humidity

It should be between 75% and 80%. Higher humidity levels favor the development of foliar diseases, especially those of fungal origin.

## Wind

It is not recommended to plant in areas exposed to winds exceeding 20 km/h (kilometers per hour), as this can damage the leaves. Winds of 50 km/h can cause the plant to bend, with significant tearing of the leaves, resulting in losses of 60% to 100%.
