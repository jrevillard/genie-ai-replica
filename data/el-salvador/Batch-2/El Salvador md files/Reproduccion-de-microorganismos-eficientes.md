## Capturing and reproducing efficient microorganisms for soil fertility restoration

## RESTORING SOIL LIFE

Healthy soil is due to the continuous growth of numerous, diverse microorganisms; primarily bacteria and fungi, which break down nutrients from organic matter provided by plants and animals, and rebuild them in forms available to the plant.

## EFFICIENT MICROORGANISMS

## 1. SOLID MICROORGANISMS

Natural ecosystems contain a wide variety of beneficial natural microorganisms that are soil and ecosystem activators, including: yeasts, actinomycetes, lactic acid-producing bacteria, and photosynthetic bacteria. These organisms break down organic matter in the soil and also decompose waste that is deposited in it. Some fix nitrogen from the atmosphere, control harmful microorganisms, increase the availability of nutrients for the plant through recycling, degrade some toxic substances, including pesticides, and produce antibiotics and other bioactive components, improving soil aggregation, among other functions.

## 2. LIQUID MICROORGANISMS

Solid microorganisms cannot be used directly, so they must undergo a process called activation, where the microorganisms are mixed with other ingredients, especially water, for optimal application and to improve their efficiency in agriculture.

Water serves as a vehicle to move the microorganisms through the soil and prevent them from dying in the process, helping us to restore soil fertility.
