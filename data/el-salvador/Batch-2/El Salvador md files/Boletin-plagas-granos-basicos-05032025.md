## Recommendations to Prevent Post-Harvest Corn Pests

- o Thoroughly clean and dry the grain; the maximum moisture content should be 12%.
- o Ensure that the containers and storage area are clean, shake the walls, ceilings, and remove any old grain before placing the new grain.
- o Store the grain away from damp areas.
- o Keep the grain stored in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight, to keep it fresh, as warm grain can easily attract certain insects.
- o Store the grain as far away from the fields as possible to prevent pests from easily traveling from the fields to the storage sites.
- o Only store whole, healthy grain.
- o Regularly inspect the grain.
