## Introduction

The Centro Nacional de Tecnología Agropecuaria y Forestal 'Enrique Álvarez Córdova' (CENTA) is dedicated to improving agricultural productivity and sustainability. This new variety of clear red bean, developed through research and field trials, is being introduced to Salvadoran farmers. This variety is the result of research conducted in experimental fields and on small and medium-sized farms. It exhibits several desirable characteristics, including high yield potential, resistance to certain diseases, and a marketable bean color.

The commercial name for this variety is in honor of Don Enrique Álvarez Córdova, a visionary leader who dedicated his life to improving the lives of small and medium-sized farmers through agricultural extension programs.

CENTA hopes that this new variety will continue to support Salvadoran farmers, helping to improve the livelihoods of rural families. Therefore, the Board of Directors, Executive Directorate, and technical and administrative staff of CENTA are proud to contribute to the food security and sovereignty of the Salvadoran population.

## Introduction

Bean cultivation is of strategic importance in El Salvador, as it is a key component of food security. Its high protein, fiber, and mineral content makes it an essential part of the diet, with an estimated consumption of 15.2 kilograms per person per year. According to the DGEA (Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock), 171,432 hectares of beans were planted in the 2013-2014 agricultural cycle, resulting in a production of 2.5 million quintals and a productivity of 15.1 qq/hectare. Demand for this crop is increasing, and production is facing challenges due to climate change and the high incidence of pests and diseases. CENTA, in collaboration with international research centers, including the El Zamorano Pan-American School, is working to develop new varieties that can adapt to these conditions.

CENTA's vision for agricultural and forestry development is based on the Quinquennial Development Plan 2014-2019, which emphasizes the need to increase food production and employment in the agricultural sector to strengthen food security.

One of the main challenges facing El Salvador is slow and uneven economic growth, resulting from a fragmented and diversified production structure with limited capacity to create jobs and improve employment opportunities in rural areas. This weak economic growth is primarily due to low productivity, high production costs, and a low level of national investment in agriculture, among other factors.

In light of the above, the Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock, through CENTA, is making this new variety available, which is the first variety released in this quinquennium. It is expected to contribute to improving the livelihoods of Salvadoran farmers, especially in challenging climatic conditions.

## Origin

The origin of the new clear red bean variety, 'CENTA EAC', is as follows:

- CENTA EAC is a cross between Negro Vaina Blanca and BCN 20-0294, developed by the Programa de Investigaciones en Frijol (PIF) at El Zamorano, Honduras. During the selection process in experimental fields and on farms, it was managed under the code SJC 730-79.
- Evaluation of the experimental line SJC 730-79 began in El Salvador in 2011 at the Centroamericano de Adaptación y Rendimiento de Grano Rojo (VIDAC Rojo) nursery, distributed by the PIF/El Zamorano.
- Due to its superior performance under biotic and abiotic factors, identified by the improvement team of the Programa Granos Básicos of CENTA and the member programs of the Centroamérica y el Caribe Bean Network, in 2013, the line SJC 730-79 was incorporated into the Centroamericano de Adaptación y Rendimiento de Grano Rojo (ECAR Rojo) trial, where its value as a promising line was confirmed due to its characteristics of upright architecture, adaptation to environments with limited moisture and high temperatures, tolerance to viruses such as the common mosaic virus and the yellow mosaic virus, high yield potential, and excellent grain quality.
- In the final stage, the line SJC 730-79 underwent a national validation process to evaluate its adaptation and response to different environments and planting seasons, resulting in superior performance compared to improved and local varieties (Table 1).

## Management

## 1. Seed

For bean planting, it is recommended to use improved seed produced by CENTA, farmers, or companies dedicated to its production, and supervised by CENTA technicians.

To ensure seed viability, it is recommended to perform germination tests before planting and to use the recommended amount of seed to achieve the desired population according to the implemented production system.

## 2. Planting Density

Between rows:

50-60 cm.

Between plants:

10-20 cm.

Seeds per mound:

2-3.

Seed quantity:

60-70 lb/acre.

## 3. Fertilization

It is recommended to follow the recommendations after a soil analysis, however, if this is not available, it is recommended to apply: 2-3 qq of formula 18-46-0 or 15-15-15 at planting, and 1 qq of Urea 25-30 days after planting.

## 4. Weed Control

- Removing weeds at the time of field preparation.

Using recommended pre-emergent and post-emergent herbicides (green leaf).

Manual control, with a cultivator or chemicals, at 15-20 and 25-30 days after planting.

## 5. Disease Control

## Common Mosaic and Golden Mosaic

Due to the low incidence of vectors (insects) that transmit these diseases in May and August, little control is required. However, regular inspections of the crop are always recommended to determine their presence.

## Common Bacteriosis

Using clean seeds, removing the previous crop's residue if it was affected; using specific pesticides in early, localized attacks.

## Rust and Angular Spot

Incorporating residue; chemical control (1-2 applications) only in cases of very early attacks (before flowering).

## 6. Insect Control

## Weevil

In severe attacks, make 1-2 applications of specific insecticides, at the beginning of flowering and one week later.

## Grain Weevils

Store in metal silos or airtight bags, after thoroughly drying the grain; fumigate if stored for a long time.
