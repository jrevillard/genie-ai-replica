## CENTA FERROMÁS BEAN

This is a red bean variety with superior nutritional value, high yield potential, and tolerance to major diseases.

## ORIGIN OF THE VARIETY

CENTA FERROMÁS is a red bean variety developed by CIAT Colombia and introduced in 2006 through the AGROSALUD project. It was initially known by the genetic code 519 DFBS 15089-22-5 and evaluated in AGROSALUD nurseries. It was validated on farmers' fields, where it demonstrated good agronomic characteristics, high yield potential, and improved nutritional quality, outperforming normal varieties in terms of iron and zinc content.

## FIELD TRIALS

In 2006, a group of lines from the AGROSALUD trial at the San Andrés 1 experimental station during the November period were evaluated, where CENTA FERROMÁS stood out for its tolerance to the yellow mosaic virus, yield potential, and bean color, achieving yields of 1508 kg/ha (23.2 qq/mz). In 2007, evaluations were conducted on a group of selected lines from 2006, originating from Nicaragua, which showed good yield potential, with average yields of 2497 kg/ha (38.4 qq/mz). In 2008, it was evaluated in the regional trials (farmers' fields) of AGROSALUD in El Salvador, obtaining average yields of 2273 kg/ha (35 qq/mz).

Starting in 2009, it was evaluated in the Advanced Line Verification Trials with high minerals (COVAMIN-09), achieving average yields of 1511 kg/ha (23.27 qqmz).

In the 2010-11 crop cycle, it was validated in farmers' fields, obtaining average yields of 1190 kg/ha (18.3 qq/mz).

Participatory evaluations with farmers and producers indicate the acceptance of CENTA FERROMÁS due to its bean color and improved nutritional content.

## RECOMMENDATIONS FOR CULTIVATION MANAGEMENT

Seed: To ensure seed quality, free of pathogens and with good germination, it is recommended to use improved seed produced by CENTA or by trained farmers to produce it locally.

Planting density: 50 cm between rows, placing one seed at 10 cm apart or 2 seeds placed 20 cm apart. The recommended amount of seed for planting one manzana (plot) is 80 pounds.

Fertilization: It is suggested to fertilize based on soil analysis. If no soil analysis is available, apply 2 qq/ha of 18-46-0 and 6-15-15 at the time of planting and 0.5 qq/ha of Urea, 25-30 days after planting. Additional foliar fertilizer application increases the vegetative and reproductive vigor of the variety and should be performed preferably before flowering.

## PEST MANAGEMENT

Weeds: Eliminate weeds through good land preparation. Manual weeding at 15-20 and 25-30 days after planting, and use pre- and post-emergent herbicides recommended.

Bean weevil: In areas with severe attacks, apply two applications of specific insecticides (at the beginning and one week after flowering).

Storage weevils: Store in airtight silos after drying the grain (13% moisture), fumigate if stored for long periods.

## DISEASE MANAGEMENT

Yellow mosaic virus and common mosaic virus: In the presence of the vectors, whitefly and aphids, no control is required, due to the tolerance of the variety.

Bacterial and fungal diseases: It is suggested to use clean seed, remove infected plants, and use recommended bactericides or fungicides.
