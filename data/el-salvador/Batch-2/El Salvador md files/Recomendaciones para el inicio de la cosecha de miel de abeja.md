## Recommendations for Starting a Honey Harvest

Before starting the honey harvest season, it is essential to prepare all the necessary equipment and materials in advance, ensuring that they are clean, in good condition, and ready for use. It is also important to take all necessary precautions to avoid any type of contamination, ensuring the quality and safety of the honey.

The harvest should only be carried out on days with favorable weather conditions: sunny, without rain, with moderate humidity, and without strong winds. It should be performed during times when the foraging bees are active in the field, as this reduces the number of bees within the colony and makes handling easier. It is important to schedule this activity during times when nectar is flowing, which reduces the risk of robbing.

It is important to avoid placing supers or other beekeeping equipment directly on the ground, as this represents a significant source of contamination that can compromise the quality of the honey and the health of the consumer.
