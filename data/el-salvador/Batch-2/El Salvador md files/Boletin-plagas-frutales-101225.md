The guava plantations are attacked by insect pests, which are described below:

- Stem borer or guava borer: The insects feed on the stem, rasping at the base of the trunks, and can even cause the death of the tree.
- Fruit fly: The flies lay their eggs on the fruits. Then, the larvae consume the pulp, causing total damage to the fruit.
- Mites: This pest causes damage by rasping the skin of the fruits, reducing their quality and affecting the selling price.

## Measures for pest control

- Remove damaged fruits and bury them.
- Install traps using attractants such as orange juice.
- Dispose of damaged tree remains to eliminate all larvae inside the stem.
- Apply lime to the base of the trees.
