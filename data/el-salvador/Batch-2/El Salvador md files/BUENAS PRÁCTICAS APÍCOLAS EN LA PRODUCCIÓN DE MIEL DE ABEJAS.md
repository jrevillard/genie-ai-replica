## 5.3.3. Hygiene

- 5.3.3.1. The application of medications must be for curative purposes, in accordance with the identified disease or pest.

- 5.3.3.2. Medications must be registered and authorized for use in bees by the competent authority of the MAG (Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock, Rural Development, Fisheries and Food). The use of the medication must be in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions or a written prescription from a veterinarian.
- 5.3.3.3. The application of a veterinary medication must be documented in accordance with item 5.5.5 of this Technical Regulation.

## 5.3.6. Hazardous Products

- 5.3.6.1. Products for pest control, cleaning, or other substances that could pose a risk to the safety of the honey, must be kept in their original packaging. When used in "daily" operations, they must be suitable and properly identified with the type of product they are.

- 5.3.6.2. These products must be stored with restricted access and must be handled only by trained personnel.

- 5.4.1. Personnel who have direct contact with beehives and honey must not have symptoms of infectious or contagious diseases or open wounds.
