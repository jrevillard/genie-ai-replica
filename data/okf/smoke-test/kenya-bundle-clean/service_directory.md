# Service Directory

The national service portal groups public services into the following
categories. Each service has an eligibility description, a required-documents
list, and a processing-time estimate.

## Identity and Civil Registration

- Birth registration
- National identity card application and renewal
- Passport application

## Digital Government Services

- eCitizen digital payments ([details](./ecitizen_digital_payments.md))
- Service booking at Huduma centres ([details](./huduma_kenya.md))

## Public Service Administration

- Civil servant records administration
- Ministry correspondence tracking ([owner](./ministry_of_public_service.md))

## Using This Directory

The directory is the entry point for assistants answering "which office handles
X" questions. Each entry links to the concept page that carries the service's
authoritative description. Where a service is delivered through the portal,
the entry also records the digital application route.
