## 2. What happened (plain words)

The prediction held almost exactly: **topN=2 uncapped the multi-passage questions.**
28 of your 42 questions expect more than one chunk; with a second pick, passage recall
jumped 13 points and the share of questions receiving *complete* evidence rose from 60%
to 79%. And the feared downside — messier answers from noisier context — did **not**
materialize: RAGAS faithfulness and context_precision actually ticked *up*. The granite
judge found the two-chunk context slightly *cleaner*, not dirtier.

The real cost is precision (0.857 → 0.524): the second pick is gold only about a third
of the time. That matters less than it looks — a redundant-but-relevant chunk costs
some tokens, not correctness, and RAGAS says the answers didn't degrade.

## 3. Verdict

**topN=2 is a clear improvement on this corpus** — better coverage, better grounding,
no answer-quality regression. It is the new candidate production cell pending the
**knee_threshold** comparison (which aims to get topN=2's coverage with adaptive pick
counts — 1 chunk when one suffices, several when needed). answer_relevancy (~0.52)
did not move and needs the **concision prompt** lever, not a retrieval lever.

**Config restored to topN=1 after the run** (production stays on the proven cell until
you pick).
