## How do we identify the fruit fly?

<!-- image -->

Fruit flies are one of the most damaging pests to national fruit production, significantly reducing the availability of healthy fruit. They are responsible for substantial economic losses and limit the ability to export fruit. Fruit fly surveillance is carried out using traps, which are useful for identifying the species present and their population in the area. They can also be used as a form of control.
