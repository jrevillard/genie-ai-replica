## Insects that are pests of corn

From the moment of planting, corn is exposed to the attacks of numerous pathogens (pests), and among the factors that favor or hinder the appearance of pests and diseases in the crop are: climate conditions, soil preparation, crop rotation, and weed control, among others. Soil pests that attack corn are common to other crops such as sorghum. The following table presents the most important soil pests in corn, in terms of economic impact.

| Common name                                                                                                                                                        | Damage                                                                                                                                                                |
|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| • Blind beetle, Chorontoco, caterpillar, white worm • Wireworm                                                                                               | Feeds on the roots and base of the stem, causing wilting and death of the plant                                                                         |
| • Corn earworm                                                                                                                                                     | Damages the grains near germination, and the roots of the seedlings bend and die.                                                                              |
| Control: Treat the seed with insecticides such as Tiametoxan 35 FS at a dose of 100 cc/25 pounds of seed, Imidacloprid 70 WS at a dose of 48 g/25 pounds of seed | Control: Treat the seed with insecticides such as Tiametoxan 35 FS at a dose of 100 cc/25 pounds of seed, Imidacloprid 70 WS at a dose of 48 g/25 pounds of seed |
