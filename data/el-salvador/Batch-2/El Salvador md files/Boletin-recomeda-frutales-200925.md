## Recommendations for preventing HLB disease in citrus

<!-- image -->

1. Obtain healthy plants free of pests. Plant healthy plants from certified nurseries in a protected environment.
2. Implement a comprehensive cultivation management strategy, with emphasis on irrigation, plant nutrition, pruning, and the destruction of infected branches, as well as control of the vector or transmitting insect of the disease.
3. Conduct regular monitoring of citrus crops, including backyard trees, and if similar symptoms of the disease are observed, immediately contact the General Directorate of Plant Health of the Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock (DGSV-MAG) and the International Regional Organization for Agricultural Health (OIRSA).
