## Identification and control of Sigatoka disease in banana cultivation

<!-- image -->

The Sigatoka disease begins with tiny spots that develop into thin, reddish-brown streaks, which merge and darken to become black. The black and dead areas dry out and turn a paler color. The spots usually intensify towards the tip of the leaf; affected leaves die within 3 or 4 weeks. It is more severe in conditions of high humidity and temperature.

## Recommendations for Sigatoka control

- Apply mineral fertilizer that provides especially Phosphorus, Potassium, and Calcium, as well as biofertilizers.
- Perform phytosanitary pruning, which involves removing damaged leaves in the banana crop, either completely or partially.
- It is recommended to perform pruning. Every month during the dry season and every 15 days during the rainy season.

- Build or restore drainage systems to prevent waterlogging in the soil.
- Control pests, weeds, and other diseases that may be present in the crop.
- The application of chemical fungicides can reduce the damage caused by Sigatoka. It is recommended to perform pruning before application.
- If there are any fallen leaves, spray them with 10% urea to inactivate the fungal spores.
