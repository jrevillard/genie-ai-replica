## Recommendations for Corn Planting

Traditionally, at the beginning of the rainy season, farmers begin preparing the soil and, subsequently, planting annual crops such as corn. The following practices are recommended for the start of planting:

- The first planting for corn is between May 25th and June 5th.
- To ensure a good corn yield, it is recommended to use certified, high-quality, and genetically pure seeds before planting.
- Ensure that the seeds have a germination rate of over 85%.
- To prevent soil-borne pest attacks, it is recommended to treat the seeds with appropriate products.
- Use soil conservation practices such as contour planting, barriers, and leaving crop residue after harvest.
- Restore drainage systems in the field.
- Conduct preventative and control measures for root rot.
