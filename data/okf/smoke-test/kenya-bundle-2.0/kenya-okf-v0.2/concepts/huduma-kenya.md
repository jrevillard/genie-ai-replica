# Huduma Service Centres

Huduma Kenya is a Vision 2030 flagship programme that provides citizen-centric
public services through one-stop-shop platforms. The physical Huduma Centres
allow citizens to access services from multiple ministries, departments, and
agencies (and in many locations county services) under one roof, reducing the
need to travel between offices.

The programme is coordinated by the Huduma Kenya Secretariat, which falls under
the [Ministry of Public Service and Human Capital Development](ministry-of-public-service.md)
(Executive Order context). Digital complement is the eCitizen portal — see
[eCitizen Digital Services & Payments](ecitizen-digital-payments.md). The
authoritative service catalogue is the [Service Directory](service-directory.md).

## Network scale (indicative)

- Approximately 57–59 operational Huduma Centres covering all 47 counties
  (Nairobi has multiple centres; some counties have more than one).
- Expansion and refurbishment continue; exact counts change over time.
- Average daily footfall is frequently reported in the tens of thousands
  (figures of ~42,000–60,000+ citizens per day have been cited in official and
  secondary reporting).
- Services deployed across the network number well over 100 physical services
  from dozens of MDAs, with continuous onboarding.

## Core service categories typically available

(Exact mix varies by centre capacity and agency deployment.)

- **Identity**: National ID application / replacement / collection, Huduma
  Namba / Digital ID support, birth & death certificate services.
- **Travel documents**: Passport application support / collection guidance
  (biometrics usually at immigration offices).
- **Transport**: NTSA driver’s licence biometrics, renewal support, related
  vehicle services.
- **Security**: Police Clearance Certificate (Certificate of Good Conduct)
  fingerprinting and collection at selected centres.
- **Business & lands**: Business registration assistance, land rates / rent
  payments, searches.
- **Social protection & tax**: NHIF/SHA, NSSF, selected KRA assisted services.
- **Other**: KUCCPS, student loans, self-help group registration, judiciary
  services at selected sites, e-GP assisted access, and more.

Many journeys begin online on eCitizen (application + payment) and finish at a
Huduma Centre (biometrics, document submission, or collection).

## Appointment booking and walk-ins

- Citizens are encouraged to book slots in advance (Service By Appointment /
  SBA) via the national portal or Huduma channels; booked customers receive a
  queue reference and experience shorter waiting times (reported reductions
  from ~45 minutes toward ~10 minutes under SBA).
- Walk-in visits are accepted subject to capacity, usually at lower priority
  than booked appointments.
- Track My Service (TMS) style status tools help citizens follow document
  readiness.

## Operating hours

- Standard government hours at most centres: approximately 08:00–17:00,
  Monday–Friday.
- Extended-hours centres (commonly 07:00–19:00) exist in major towns. Recent
  public lists have included (among others): Nairobi GPO, City Square,
  Eastleigh, Makadara, Kibra; Thika, Nakuru, Eldoret, Nyeri, Embu, Meru,
  Laikipia, Kisii, Kitale, Kericho, Kakamega, Kisumu, Mombasa, Bungoma,
  Migori, Makueni, and additional sites as announced. Always verify current
  hours on hudumakenya.go.ke before travelling.

## Contact and escalation channels

Huduma Contact and Tele-Counselling Centre:

- Telephone: **1919**
- WhatsApp (cited in programme materials): 0747 191919
- Email: info@hudumakenya.go.ke
- Social: @Hudumakenya (X/Twitter), Facebook, Instagram, YouTube

Hours typically 07:00–21:00 weekdays and 08:00–18:00 weekends/public holidays
(confirm current schedule).

Unresolved counter cases escalate to the centre supervisor and, where a
departmental decision is required, to the liaison officer of the responsible
ministry. Overall service-delivery standards and the Huduma programme itself
sit under the [Ministry of Public Service and Human Capital Development](ministry-of-public-service.md).
