## Yellow Mosaic Virus or Yellow Vitis in Bean Cultivation

<!-- image -->

## Symptoms of the disease:

The leaves exhibit a bright yellow color. If the plants are affected before flowering, there is premature flower abortion and deformation of the pod. The seeds show spots and deformities, leading to a decrease in weight. Plants affected in very early stages show severe dwarfism and do not produce pods.

## Recommendations:

- Implement effective control of the whitefly.
- Plant tolerant varieties.
- Frequent monitoring.
- Remove affected plants upon detecting the first symptoms.
- Do not plant near cucumber, tomato, chili, and papaya crops.
- Use potassium soap at a concentration of 50 gr/l of water.
- Use products such as: flupyradifuron 20 SL (1 liter per manzana), ethioprole + imidacloprid (1.4 liters per manzana), thiametoxam 25 WG (300 grams per manzana).
