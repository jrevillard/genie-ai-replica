## Aphids transmit diseases to chili crop

<!-- image -->

Aphids, also known as aphids, damage a wide variety of horticultural crops. The symptoms are discoloration, damaged and yellowing leaves. Aphids live in colonies or groups, specifically on the underside or back of the leaves and on shoots, invading the most mature leaves.

The insects feed by sucking the sap from the plant, but in doing so, they transmit the disease to the crop. In addition, they secrete sugary substances in which a fungus known as fumagina grows, which causes darkening of the leaves.

Aphids act as vectors or transmitters of diseases to the chili crop, such as the mosaic virus, tobacco virus, among others.
