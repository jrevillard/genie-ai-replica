## **What is AgroGenio AI?**

AgroGenio AI is an advanced AI assistant designed specifically for the agricultural sector in El Salvador. Using a technique called Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), it actively searches a specialized database of **229 documents** from CENTA (National Center for Agricultural, Livestock, and Forestry Technology) covering crop cultivation, animal production, pest management, and sustainable farming practices. It finds the most relevant, up-to-date information before answering your question, ensuring accurate, research-based agricultural guidance.

## **What agricultural topics can AgroGenio AI help me with?**

AgroGenio AI provides comprehensive coverage across El Salvador's agricultural sectors:

* **Grain Crops:** Corn (maíz), beans (frijol), rice (arroz), and sorghum (sorgo) - including varieties, planting, pest control, fertilization, and storage
* **Fruit Trees:** Avocado, mango, plantain, papaya, cacao, and more - including cultivation guides, pest alerts, and post-harvest handling

## **What is NDVI and why is it important for my farm?**

NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) is a satellite-derived measure of vegetation health and density. In AgroGenio AI:

* **Scale:** NDVI values range from -1 to +1, with higher values indicating healthier, more dense vegetation
* **Interpretation:** Values above 0.6 typically indicate healthy, vigorous crops; 0.4-0.6 suggest moderate health; and below 0.4 may indicate stress, disease, or poor vegetation
* **Monitoring:** Track NDVI trends over time to identify improving or declining crop conditions before they become visible to the naked eye
* **Regional Comparison:** Compare your department's vegetation health against neighboring areas to understand broader environmental conditions
* **Early Warning:** Declining NDVI can indicate water stress, nutrient deficiencies, or disease onset, allowing for earlier intervention
* **Applicable to All Crops:** Useful for monitoring grain fields, fruit orchards, vegetable plots, and pasture health

## **Why might my agricultural question not return useful results?**

Several reasons could explain limited or irrelevant results:

* **Sector Not Covered:** The system focuses on the major agricultural sectors in El Salvador (grains, fruits, vegetables, bees, poultry, aquaculture, livestock). Questions about minor crops or exotic species may have limited information
* **Query Too General:** Questions like "Help with my farm" or "Why are my plants dying?" are too vague. Specify the crop, animal, symptoms, location, and conditions
* **Out-of-Season Topic:** Asking about dry-season cattle management during the rainy season may not yield the most relevant seasonal recommendations
* **Limited Local Data:** Some departments may have less monitoring data than others, affecting chart availability
* **Language Mismatch:** While the system has both English and Spanish documents, some technical guides may only be available in Spanish
* **System Processing:** During high-usage periods, there may be brief delays. If you see "Thinking...", please wait a moment

**Tips for Better Results:**
- Select the appropriate category from the service tree before asking
- Use common crop/animal names in either English or Spanish (maíz/corn, frijol/beans, gallinas/chickens)
- Describe visible symptoms you can see in the field or facility
- Mention your department and current season/production cycle
- Be specific about your production system (irrigated vs. rain-fed, free-range vs. confined, etc.)

## **What is the difference between "Document Name" and "File Name"?**

* **Document Name:** This is the user-friendly, descriptive title of the agricultural guide or bulletin (e.g., "Corn Planting and Variety Guide" or "Bulletin: Fall Armyworm Control in Corn")
* **File Name:** This is the technical file name stored in the system (e.g., "Guia_Maiz_Verificacion_2018.pdf" or "Boletin_Gusano_Cogollero_2024.md")

Displaying both helps you understand exactly what source material you're viewing. The Document Name describes the content in plain language, while the File Name helps with technical reference and can indicate the date or version of bulletin documents.

## **Why does clicking an agricultural document open a new tab with a complex URL?**

Your agricultural queries and document access are secure and require authentication. The system uses this security approach for several reasons:

* **Protected Content:** CENTA technical guides, variety bulletins, and agricultural bulletins contain proprietary information that requires authentication
* **Secure Token Delivery:** A simple link cannot carry the necessary security token. When you click, the application fetches the document in the background using your authenticated credentials
* **Temporary Local Access:** The system creates a temporary, local URL for your browser to open the document
* **Security Protection:** This ensures you can view the agricultural guides while ensuring CENTA's valuable knowledge remains protected and properly attributed to the authoritative sources

This means you can confidently access CENTA's comprehensive agricultural knowledge base while ensuring the information remains secure and properly sourced.
