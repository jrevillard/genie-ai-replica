## What are the tomato fruit pests?

<!-- image -->

## · Tomato fruit worm

The larvae initially feed on the leaves, then bore into the fruit, allowing pathogens to enter.

## · False measuring worm

The larvae reach a length of 30 mm, are green in color with lateral and dorsal green and white stripes; sometimes with black thoracic legs. They feed on the fruit, facilitating the infection of pathogens such as bacteria and fungi.

## Recommendations for managing the worms

- Conduct sampling
- Apply Bacillus thuringiensis insecticide, 0.7 to 1.4 l/ha. This product should be applied when larvae are observed in their early stages.
- Teflubenzuron (Nomolt), 0.15 l/ha
- Lufenuron (Match), 275-350 cc/mz
