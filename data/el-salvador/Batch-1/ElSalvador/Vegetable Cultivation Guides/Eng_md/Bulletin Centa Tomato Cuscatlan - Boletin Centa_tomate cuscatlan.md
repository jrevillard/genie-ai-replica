## CENTA CUSCATLÁN - CC

A tomato variety with free pollination and tolerance to the begomovirus complex, transmitted by the whitefly.

## I. INTRODUCTION

One of the main vegetables with the highest consumption nationally is the tomato; however, the producer faces multiple problems if they want to achieve good production, especially because many varieties are susceptible to pests.

Another limitation is the lack of seed availability and the high prices for acquiring it, which translates into a disadvantage for small farmers. In both cases, the quality of the genetic material is unknown, which carries the risk of being susceptible to the begomovirus complex, caused by the whitefly, and consequently, the productions are minimal, which leads to a decrease in planting areas due to low yields.

In the face of this unfavorable situation for the producer, CENTA, through the Hortalizas Program, has developed a new tomato variety for culinary use, with free pollination, which has good potential for yield, is grown in open fields, and which also allows the producer to obtain seeds for new plantings.

<!-- image -->

## II. ORIGIN

This tomato variety for culinary use comes from the World Vegetable Center, based in Taiwan. In 2013, it was introduced to El Salvador from Nicaragua and evaluated in a group of 29 varieties collected from Central American and Panama; subsequently, the selection and evaluation process began in fields of producers in 2014.

After evaluation and adaptation, in 2015, the validation and seed production process began in fields of producers throughout the country.

## III. RESEARCH STRATEGY

Between 2015 and 2016, the validation was carried out in five municipalities in El Salvador, which were: Zapotitán and Sacacoyo (La Libertad), Rosario (Cuscatlán), Armenia (Sonsonate), and Atiquizaya (Ahuachapán), where it showed an average yield of 19.26 t.ha-1. In all locations, it was compared with a local variety known as 1098 (Table 2).

After completing the validation process, a survey of opinion was conducted with 100% of the producers in the five municipalities; 82% confirmed that it is a tomato variety with good quality, tolerant to begomovirus, and that it meets the expectations of producers and consumers, culminating in its release and naming as CENTA CUSCATLÁN-CC.

## V. CROP MANAGEMENT

Seed: To ensure the success of the crop, it is recommended that the seed has 95% germination.

Soil: It is recommended to prepare the soil well, one pass with a plow, two passes with a rake, and a furrow of more than 30 cm deep during the rainy season and 20 cm during the dry season.

Spacing: 1.50 m between rows x 0.50 m between plants, during the rainy season; 1.20 m between rows x 0.50 m between plants, during the dry season.

Fertilization: Before any fertilization plan is carried out, it is recommended to carry out a soil analysis. In general, the plant requires the following amounts:

<!-- image -->

N: 160 kg.ha- 1 ; P 2 O 5 : 80 kg.ha- 1 ; K 2 O: 160 kg.ha- 1 ; Ca: 150 kg.ha- 1 ; Mg: 25 kg.ha- 1 ; S: 22 kg.ha- 1 .

Pest and disease management: Carry out preventive applications every week with environmentally friendly products, starting from transplant.

Harvest: The harvest time is when a slight red color appears at the base of the fruit (85-90 days after planting in trays).
