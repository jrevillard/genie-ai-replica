Metric glossary — how each score is computed

Two families: deterministic anchor (chunk-level, content-hash matched) and LLM-judged RAGAS (semantic). Both paths are needed — neither sees the other's failures.

Anchor (deterministic)

  • chunk_recall — fraction of gold chunks the reranker kept. The headline number.
  • chunk_precision — fraction of selected chunks that are gold (vs noise).
  • retrieval_recall — fraction of gold chunks in the candidate pool before reranking. Diagnoses retriever vs reranker: low = retriever miss.
  • passage_recall — same as retrieval_recall but counted at passage level (parent doc of a gold chunk). Captures partial credit.
  • recall@k — fraction of gold in top-k of selected (k=1,3,5,10). Rank-aware. Recall@1 = "did the right chunk come first?".
  • ndcg@k — NDCG with binary relevance, normalized by IDCG. Position-weighted: gold at rank 1 contributes more than at rank 5.

RAGAS (LLM-judged)

  • faithfulness — is the answer grounded in the retrieved contexts? (Hallucination detector.)
  • context_precision — are relevant chunks ranked above irrelevant ones?
  • context_recall — do the retrieved contexts cover the reference answer?
  • answer_relevancy — does the answer address the question? (Embedding-based.)

What to read

  • factor=0.0006 (current, validated): avg_sel 9.12, faithfulness 0.655 — LLM sees ~9 grounded chunks per query.
  • factor=0.0020 (T10 crater): avg_sel 3.26 (−64%), faithfulness 0.506 (−16.5pt).
  • Root cause: context_decay_cost = factor × token_count dominates adaptive selection. Higher factor = stricter threshold = fewer chunks kept = less context for LLM.
  • Smoking gun: vars.yml:118-119 notes CONTEXT_DECAY_FACTOR=0.0006 was tuned for our MD chunk_size=2000. Chunker tightened to 1400 without re-calibrating.
  • Lesson: offline F1 ≠ live RAGAS. Always validate live before deploying offline champion.

Three takeaways from the Sept 23 → Oct 02 cycle (K=32 apples-to-apples):
1. BGE query prefix wrapper + re-calibrated reranker (T13b-K=32) is the new champion: factor 0.0006 (unchanged), thresh -0.25→-1.0, K=32 (validated). RAGAS context_precision +29.6%, context_recall +11.6%, answer_relevancy +4.1%, faithfulness +3.7% vs T11 baseline — all 4 RAGAS metrics beat baseline.
2. The wrapper + re-cal are stacking fixes, not replacements. T12-K=32 (wrapper alone) regressed RAGAS despite gains in anchor quality (chunk_precision +2.4pp) — re-cal was required to recover the wrapper-induced coverage losses (passage_recall 30→49/74). Always re-calibrate the reranker when changing upstream retrieval behavior.
3. Oct 01 K=20 sweep was misleading on net: T13a at K=20 won faithfulness (+4.5%) but lost answer_relevancy (−4.1%) and the K=20 base was a transient override. The Oct 02 K=32 apples-to-apples sweep is the validated live state. See §13.5 of the report for the K=20/K=32 drift timeline.
