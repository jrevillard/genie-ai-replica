## Wax Moth Damage to Beehives

The wax moth larva is a parasite that feeds, defecates, and lives within the honeycomb. The presence of this parasite can lead to the abandonment of the hive and the loss of constructed honeycomb. Diagnosis of the wax moth is made through direct observation of the larvae and adults within the hive.

## Pest Damage

Moths create galleries within the honeycomb and cover them with a thread-like substance similar to spider silk. They feed on the honeycomb and anything within it, causing significant losses to the hive due to the complete destruction of the honeycomb.

## Prevention

Storing constructed honeycomb should ensure proper ventilation to prevent infestation by moths. Maintaining strong hives and reducing the size of the entrance to orphaned, new, or weak hives; as well as removing and storing honeycomb that the bees do not use during periods of scarcity.
