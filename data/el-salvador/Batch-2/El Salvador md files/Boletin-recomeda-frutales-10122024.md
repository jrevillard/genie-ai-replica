## Recommendations for the Management of Fruit Fly Traps

<!-- image -->

The fruit fly is one of the pests that causes the most damage to national fruit production, significantly reducing the availability of healthy fruit. Using traps is a method of control and monitoring fruit flies. Traps are structures that attract and capture these insects. They should be placed in a location near the fruit on the tree and in a central location on the tree.

The following are recommendations for managing fruit fly traps:

Place them in a shaded area to prevent the contents from evaporating.

Ensure that leaves or branches do not obstruct the entrance.

<!-- image -->

<!-- image -->

Place them in a location where air circulates to enhance the effectiveness of the attractant.

In papaya plantations, hooks can be attached to stakes and placed alongside the papayas, with the trap installed near the fruit. They can also be placed on neighboring trees.

Plastic traps should be replaced when they become opaque.

The number of traps to use depends primarily on the population of fruit flies: between 5 and 20 per tree, depending also on the available resources. A good approach is to place most of the traps on the periphery of the crop and some distributed throughout the orchard.
