## Monitoring pests and diseases in orchards

<!-- image -->

Monitoring crops is a fundamental practice for integrated pest and disease management, which involves the periodic inspection of the crop to detect the presence of pests or diseases and assess the damage, allowing for decisions to be made to prevent, avoid losses, and control pests in fruit orchards.

## · Visual inspection:

The farmer or technician should inspect the field or crop area to look for signs of pests or diseases in the plants, such as damage to leaves, fruits, or stems, the presence of insects, or symptoms of disease.

## · Use of traps:

Traps can be used to catch pests, using yellow, blue, white, sticky, or light-based traps.

## · Identification of pests and diseases:

The type of pest or disease is identified, using field guides, experts, or plant parasitology laboratory analysis, such as CENTA.

The National Center for Agricultural and Forestry Technology "Enrique Álvarez Córdova" (CENTA), through its plant parasitology laboratory, is responsible for making diagnoses of pests and diseases in agricultural crops for farmers. Providing recommendations for the management and control of pests and diseases that damage crops, focused on integrated management.

The laboratory specialists use technologies and techniques to detect and combat pests and diseases. In addition, they offer a wide range of specialized services that guarantee the health of your crops. The laboratory is a very important and indispensable support in the food security of the country, due to the accurate diagnoses and recommendations for the management and control of pests and diseases, technical assistance in the field for agricultural producers, which helps to avoid greater losses due to pests and diseases in agricultural production in the country.
