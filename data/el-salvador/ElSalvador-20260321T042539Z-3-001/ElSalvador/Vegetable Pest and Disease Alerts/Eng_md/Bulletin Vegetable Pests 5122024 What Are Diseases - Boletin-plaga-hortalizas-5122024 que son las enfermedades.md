## What are pests and diseases?

<!-- image -->

A good farmer should know how to identify the pests and diseases of their crops, in order to control them. Some living organisms, such as insects, fungi, bacteria, or viruses, cause health problems in the plant, and the producer must learn to recognize them.

<!-- image -->

Insects can be seen on parts of the plants or in the soil. They damage the plants by chewing on the leaves, roots, or fruit; by sucking the sap from the leaves, trunk, fruit, or as transmitters of disease.

<!-- image -->

<!-- image -->

Fungi are very small and attack any part of the plant. A sign of the presence of fungi can be a powdery substance under the leaves, rotting spots, black spots on the stem, withered or rotten leaves and roots. Fungi spread mechanically (by the use of tools, equipment, and contaminated personnel); through rainwater when it splashes the leaves with soil and carried by the wind from one plant to another.

Bacteria and viruses cause the decomposition of roots, stems and leaves that are twisted or scratched with streaks, black spots and other symptoms. They spread through water, soil and infected plants.
