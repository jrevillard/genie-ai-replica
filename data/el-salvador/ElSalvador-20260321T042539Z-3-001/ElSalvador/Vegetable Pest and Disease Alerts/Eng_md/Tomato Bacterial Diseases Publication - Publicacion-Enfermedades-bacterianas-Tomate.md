## MEASURES FOR CONTROL

-  Use of resistant varieties (seeds or healthy seedlings).
-  Disinfection of tools with, a part of bleach in 9 parts of water to prevent subsequent infections.
-  Carry out treatment on the soil and irrigation water prior to analysis.
-  Apply copper-based products as a preventative measure.
-  Weed control.
-  Crop rotation.
-  Suggested chemical products for controlling the bacteria:
-  TREPTOMYCIN, OXITETRACYCLINE 100, at a dose of 340 grams per 200 liters of water.
-  COPPER HYDROXIDE 101 at a dose of 2 to 3 Lb. / Mz.
-  METHYLTHIOUREA, OXITETRACYCLINE 80 WP at a dose of 1.12 Kg / Mz.
-  Alternate them to avoid pathogen resistance.
-  Carry out the sprays every 10 days, performing a maximum of three applications.
-  Other ORGANIC options for controlling the bacteria:
-  EXTRACT OF MIMOSSA TENUIFLORA 80 % C (For example: MIMOTEN) at a dose of 3.0 cc / 1 liter, perform the applications 5 every day.
-  ORGANIC FUNGICIDE AND BACTERICIDE BASED ON VEGETAL EXTRACTS, EXTRACT OF GOVERNADORA (Larrea tridentata) 22 %. More DILUENT, SPUMANTE AND EXTRACTANT OR SOLVENT 78 %, (For example: BELA PLUS) at a dose of 1.5 liters / 100 liters of water at a rate of 10 to 15 ml to the base of the stem on wet (after irrigation) and repeat at 15 or 20 days after.

## IMPORTANCE

Among the main diseases that affect the tomato crop are those caused by bacteria, which are the most frequent and damaging to this crop. They cause significant economic losses for farmers. Therefore, the aim is to make known tools that help to identify these diseases in time and to find treatments that reduce their incidence.

Principal bacterial diseases that affect the tomato crop in the fields of farmers:

## Erwinia sp.

Cause of soft rot in the lower part of the stem, causing wilting and death, attacking at any stage of the crop.

## Ralstonia (Pseudomonas) solanaceaarum)

Responsible for the general wilting of the plant

Initial state, begins with wilting of the top in the hot hours.

Advanced state, the wilting is generalized throughout the plant, it does not recover and dies.
