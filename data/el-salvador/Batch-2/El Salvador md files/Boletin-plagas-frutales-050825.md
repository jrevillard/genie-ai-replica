## Fruit Fly Damage and Control

<!-- image -->

## Damage caused by fruit flies

Fruit flies (Ceratitis capitata and Anastrepha spp.) affect various types of fruit during ripening, causing a decay that renders the fruit unusable for both fresh consumption and the food industry. Ripe or fermenting fruits are the ones that attract adult flies for their egg-laying. Each individual fruit is visited by a single female. The biological cycle can be completed in 20 to 50 days.

## Recommendations for controlling fruit flies

- a. Bury deeply in the soil (at least 10 centimeters) and/or destroy affected fruits to prevent the spread of adult flies and subsequent damage to healthy fruits.
- b. Place traps with fermented fruit juice in plastic bottles, which are punctured at the top to facilitate the entry of adult flies, thus trapping them. It is suggested to hang these traps from the trees or bushes in the crop to reduce the number of adult flies.
