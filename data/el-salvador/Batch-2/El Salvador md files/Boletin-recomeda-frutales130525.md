## Recommendations for Planting

<!-- image -->

First, prepare the soil by clearing it, then perform the digging. It is recommended to dig holes with dimensions that can vary from 0.40 meters (width) x 0.40 m (length) x 0.40 m (height) or 0.60 x 0.60 x 0.60 m. The spacing depends on the crop.

The planting hole should be filled with a mixture of soil and organic matter or compost. The plant should be transplanted with care to avoid damaging the roots, especially when removing the bag. For medium-sized mango varieties, a spacing of 5 x 5 meters can be used, while for tall varieties, a spacing of 7 x 7 m can be used, in rows, with a population of 400 and 204 plants per hectare.
