## Cercosporiosis disease in the chili crop

<!-- image -->

## What damage does it cause in the crop?

Cercosporiosis is more frequent during the rainy season, it presents circular, light gray, and dark-edged leaf spots. Severe infections can cause defoliation and lead to a reduction in yields. Defoliation causes damage to the fruits due to the sun. In humid conditions, the fungus can grow on the lesions. When large lesions dry, they often break, and the dry tissue falls off.

The disease is caused by a fungus that develops best in high humidity and warm temperatures. The first symptoms appear in the flowering stage. It is transmitted by wind to re-establish in other plants.

## Control for the disease

- Development of good agricultural practices that promote the best plant development and good production.
- Use of clean or certified seeds.
- Maintain clean drainage in the field.
