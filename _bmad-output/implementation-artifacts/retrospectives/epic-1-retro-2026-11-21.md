### Process lessons (what worked, for Epic 2)

- **Per-story squash + native gates worked.** One branch per story, squash-merged into the umbrella with a `chore-story-N-done` bookkeeping merge: per-story diffs stayed reviewable, sprint-status stayed machine-checkable (10/10 `done`), and the OPEA-free gate + equivalence suite ran in CI continuously from story 1.4 on.
- **Record-before-port paid off.** Because V02's fixtures existed before the port (story 1.1 → 1.6–1.8), the equivalence suite had recorded expected values to assert against — it caught the too-long-turn divergence in story 1.9's review and pins the four deliberate deviations today.
- **Per-story review triage logs worked.** Stories 1.4–1.9 carry formal review-round tables with verified dispositions; the aggregate pass found only one production-impossible defect in ~46k added lines, and the per-story rounds had already caught the rest.
- **What to change:** the four action items above (status convention, refresh cadence, fake-surface convention, V05 §9 dispositions).
