## 🌾 General Advisory Notes

Farmers and agricultural stakeholders are advised to:

- Follow updated government agricultural and safety guidelines  
- Maintain proper hygiene and field sanitation  
- Avoid working in large groups when unnecessary  
- Regularly clean farming tools and machinery  
- Monitor weather forecasts closely during the monsoon season  

---

## 🌦️ Seasonal Overview (May–August)

- This period typically includes **pre-monsoon and monsoon conditions**
- Expect:
  - Increasing rainfall frequency
  - High humidity
  - সম্ভাব্য thunderstorms and gusty winds
- Temperature:
  - Warm to hot conditions early (May–June)
  - Slight cooling during peak monsoon (July–August)

---

## 🚜 Key Agricultural Guidance

### Rice (Boro / Aman Preparation)
- Maintain proper irrigation and drainage  
- Monitor for pests and fungal diseases due to humidity  
- Apply fertilizers based on soil condition  

### Vegetables
- Ensure proper drainage to prevent waterlogging  
- Use pest control measures during humid conditions  

### Livestock
- Keep shelters dry and ventilated  
- Ensure clean drinking water  

### Fisheries
- Monitor pond water quality during heavy rainfall  
- Prevent overflow and contamination  

---

## ⚠️ Weather Precautions

- Prepare for **heavy rainfall events**
- Strengthen crop support structures
- Use early warning systems where available
