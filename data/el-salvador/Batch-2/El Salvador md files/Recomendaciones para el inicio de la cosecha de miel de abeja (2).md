## Recommendations for the Start of Honey Harvesting

<!-- image -->

## Use of Personal Protective Equipment

The protective equipment consists of a veil, overalls, gloves, and boots. The beekeeper must always use clean equipment, and it is recommended to wash it after use and store it in places free from contaminants such as agrochemicals, fumigants, etc.

## Selection, Loading, and Transport of Honey Frames

- Select frames with mature honey.
- To remove bees from honey frames, a bee brush or manual shaking can be used. Do not use repellents or chemicals to remove bees from frames, as these contaminate the honey.
- Honey frames should be placed in boxes located on bee-proof trays (previously washed), and the boxes should be covered with an outer lid to prevent contamination with dust, insects, and robber bees.
- Never place the frames directly on the floor, as this increases the risk of contamination.
- Bee-proof trays should be made of stainless steel or coated with beeswax. The honey recovered in the bee-proof trays should not be mixed with the extracted honey.
- If the harvest is carried out at a honey extraction facility more than 300 meters from the apiary, the boxes containing the honey frames should be protected with a clean tarp.
- Frames should not be selected for harvest that contain open or closed brood.
