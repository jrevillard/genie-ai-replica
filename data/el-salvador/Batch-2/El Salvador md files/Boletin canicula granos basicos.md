## Recommendations for staple crops during the heatwave

The heatwave is a period characterized by decreased rainfall and higher temperatures, typically occurring between the second half of July and the first few days of August. During the heatwave, droughts can occur, leading to water stress for staple crop cultivation.

- If you have irrigation available, irrigate the crops during the cooler parts of the day, before 10 am and after 4 pm.
- Incorporate straw (mulch) into the soil to conserve soil moisture.
- Control and do not eliminate weeds.
- Strengthen monitoring of pests and diseases in the crop.
- Apply amino acids to the crops to address the stress caused by drought.
- Apply fertilizer to the soil to help the crop recover.
