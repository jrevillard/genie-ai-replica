## Basic Grains Program

The Basic Grains Program is dependent on the Technology and Research Development Management, and is responsible for generating technologies in the germplasm of basic grains with the potential for high yield and resistance/tolerance to the main biotic and abiotic factors, as well as being biofortified, suitable for use by small, medium and large producers. Similarly, technologies for integrated crop management are researched and validated. All of this is carried out through the participation of specialists in the areas of corn, beans, rice and sorghum, who coordinate with other units of the Management, such as: Seed Technology, Experimental Stations, Germplasm Bank and laboratories.

## Main objectives

To contribute to improving food security and quality through the improvement of the production and productivity of basic grains in different environments in El Salvador.

Select varieties and/or hybrids of basic grains with high potential for yield and high protein quality (biofortified) and adaptable to various agroecological environments in El Salvador.

Select varieties and/or hybrids of basic grains with tolerance to the effects caused by climate change in our country.

Reduce environmental pollution and production costs through the reduced use of pesticides.

Generate technologies for integrated crop management in basic grains.

## Offers

1. Varieties of the different basic grain categories through research.
2. Specialized technical assistance in basic grains.
3. Training.
4. Generation of appropriate technology.
5. Development of means for disseminating technology.

## Coordinated work

The Basic Grains Program coordinates its actions with:

- Small, medium and large producers.
- Cooperation organizations such as FAO, IICA, KoLFACI, HarvestPlus.
- Academic institutions: ENA and national and foreign universities.
- International Research Centers: CIAT (International Center for Tropical Agriculture), CIMMYT (International Center for Maize and Wheat Improvement), and Universidad Panamericana Zamorano.

- Rice variety CENTA 'A - RAZ'.
- Biofortified rice variety 'CENTA A Nutremás'.
- Technology: double row system in yellow corn hybrid 'CENTA H - CAS'.
- Technology: management of the yellow sorghum pest through the use of bio-rational and synthetic products.
