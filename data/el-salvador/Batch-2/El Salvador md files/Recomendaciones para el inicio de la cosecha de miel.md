## Recommendations for the Start of Honey Harvesting

<!-- image -->

Producing good honey and meeting consumer preferences are key goals for Salvadoran beekeepers. Honey harvesting should take place in a clean and enclosed space, which can be either mobile or fixed, depending on the producer's capabilities. The space should ensure isolation from the environment, with adequate protection against dust, and allow for proper cleaning, while also preventing the entry of bees, pests, and rodents.

In November, honey harvesting season typically begins, so the following recommendations are suggested:
