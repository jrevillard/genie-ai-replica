Chart 3 · T10 crater (factor=0.0020)

What NOT to do: offline F1 champion + live deploy without RAGAS validation. factor=0.0020 vs factor=0.0006 (validated).
Avg_sel per config
RAGAS faithfulness

What to read

  • factor=0.0006 (current, validated): avg_sel 9.12, faithfulness 0.655 — LLM sees ~9 grounded chunks per query.
  • factor=0.0020 (T10 crater): avg_sel 3.26 (−64%), faithfulness 0.506 (−16.5pt).
  • Root cause: context_decay_cost = factor × token_count dominates adaptive selection. Higher factor = stricter threshold = fewer chunks kept = less context for LLM.
  • Smoking gun: vars.yml:118-119 notes CONTEXT_DECAY_FACTOR=0.0006 was tuned for our MD chunk_size=2000. Chunker tightened to 1400 without re-calibrating.
  • Lesson: offline F1 ≠ live RAGAS. Always validate live before deploying offline champion.

Three takeaways from the Sept 23 → Sept 30 cycle:
1. XML prompt + section-aware chunker delivers +0.122 faithfulness (≈2.5× actual SE for n=39-42) — well-validated, keep.
