# Public Service Directory

This document indexes major government services mapped to the [eCitizen](./ecitizen_digital_payments.md) portal and [Huduma Centres](./huduma_kenya.md). Availability of specific services may vary by centre; appointments are often required (e.g., via appointment.hudumakenya.go.ke).

## Transport \& Driving (NTSA)

* Driver's License Application \& Renewal
* Smart Driving Licence issuance / biometric capture
* Vehicle Registration and Transfer of Ownership
* Public Service Vehicle (PSV) Badge Applications
* Motor Vehicle Inspection Bookings
* Logbook services (duplicate, change of ownership/address)

## Identity \& Immigration

* **Civil Registration:** Birth and Death Certificates (issuance and duplicates)
* **Immigration:** Passport applications/renewals, replacement of lost passports, Temporary Travel Documents, Visas for foreign visitors
* **National Registration Bureau:** Issuance of National IDs (including Maisha Card / 2nd Generation), replacements, updates, tracking

## Business \& Professional Registration

* **Business Registration Service (BRS):** Company registration, Business Name Search and Reservation, Annual Returns Filing
* **AGPO Registration:** Access to Government Procurement Opportunities (certifying youth, women, and PWDs for public tenders)
* Professional memberships and related certifications

## Tax \& Law Enforcement

* **Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA):** KRA PIN registration/recovery, Tax Compliance Certificates, Income and VAT filing support
* **Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI):** Police Clearance Certificates (Certificate of Good Conduct) for citizens and foreigners; Police Abstracts

## Social Services \& Healthcare

* **Social Health Authority (SHA) / NHIF successor:** Health insurance registration and contributions
* **National Social Security Fund (NSSF):** Pension and welfare program tracking
* **HELB:** Student loan applications, repayment statements, compliance/clearance certificates
* Other: Uwezo Fund, Women Enterprise Fund, Unclaimed Financial Assets Authority services

## Additional Notes

* Many services support both self-service (eCitizen) and assisted access at Huduma Centres.
* County-level services (e.g., business permits, land rates) available at select centres.
* Contact Huduma 1919 for real-time guidance on which centre offers a specific service.
