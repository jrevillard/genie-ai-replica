## Bacterial disease that harms bees in hives

American foulbrood is a serious disease for bees, especially for young bees. The larvae dry out, forming crusty patches on the walls and bottom of the cells, leading to the death of the bees.

## Cause of the disease

- Beekeepers can spread the disease by working with contaminated hives, frames, and equipment; when expanding populations with infected larvae; or when transporting sick bees.
- A lack of food, especially pollen, honey, and a shortage of water, weakens the hive's resistance and promotes the development of the disease.
- When cleaning the cells, bees can transmit spores to new larvae. It can also be transmitted through stored honey, and with pollen preserved in contaminated honey.
