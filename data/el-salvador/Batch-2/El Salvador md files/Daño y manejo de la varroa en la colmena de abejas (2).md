## Damage and management of varroa in the beehive

<!-- image -->

## What is varroa?

It is an external parasitic mite that can affect the queen, worker bees, drones, and their offspring, causing stress in the bees. This parasite feeds on bees and, in turn, also acts as a vector for the introduction of other diseases. It is cream, brown, or orange in color.

## What damage does the pest cause to bees?

Varroa parasitizes bees by feeding on them, primarily located on the upper part of the thorax and abdomen, and can even cause their death. It is important to regularly check for varroa in the hives.

## Control and management

- Maintain strong hives, only breed drones when necessary, and avoid contact between healthy hives and boxes, frames, and any other objects used in infested hives.
- Control robbing, avoid using feeders in infested areas.
- Replace old frames.
- Maintain good hygiene in the hive.

## During rainy months:

- Control the ventilation of the hive to prevent the build-up of humidity in the internal walls.
- Check the condition of the hive, especially the larvae.
