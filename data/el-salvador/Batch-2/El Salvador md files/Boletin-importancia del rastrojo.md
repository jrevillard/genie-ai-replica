## Maintaining crop residue is a good practice in fields

<!-- image -->

Crop residue refers to the leftover material after harvesting crops. It plays an important and multifaceted role in the soil. It helps to retain moisture and protect the soil.

A soil without ground cover is vulnerable and exposed to drought, rain, and wind. Ground cover protects the soil from these weather events; furthermore, it allows rainwater to be stored in the soil, preventing erosion and runoff.

Both dead and living ground cover can be used, such as green manure that is associated with maize, either temporarily or permanently, such as scattered trees in fields and the ongoing maintenance of crop residue. It is recommended to avoid burning crop residue in fields and to leave it spread on the soil.
