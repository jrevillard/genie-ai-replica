## Recommendations for Handling Sorghum Grain After Harvest

The sorghum harvest begins with selecting mature grain heads that are free of pests and diseases.

- Spread the sorghum heads out until they can be easily separated by pressing them with your fist.
- Threshing involves striking the dry sorghum heads to separate and remove the grain from the head.
- Clean, blow, and check the moisture content of the grain, which should be between 10 and 12%.
- The grain should not contain soil, broken grains, or foreign matter, as these particles often have a higher moisture content, are prone to mold and mycotoxins, and are easily affected by insects.
- Do not mix old grain with new grain.
- Use a sealed silo, barrels, or plastic bags (without air or water exchange).
- If using a granary, it should be indoors, on a raised platform, and in the shade.
- Cure the grain to prevent damage from pests such as weevils. Use garlic, onions, or a chemical product.
