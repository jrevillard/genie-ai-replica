## Recommendations for improving summer jocote production

In El Salvador, CENTA recommends carrying out the following activities during the summer jocote flowering season:

- Apply fertilizer to the soil with a dose of 534 grams (g) of triple 15 per tree after the harvest, and then every 4 months.
- Apply products based on auxins (Indole-3-acetic acid) and plant hormones (Gibberellins) to promote flower setting, even if the tree has no leaves.
- If there is a presence of mites, apply amitraz to combat spider mites (red spider mites) that damage the fruits, stems, and leaves.
- Use different products for the control of aphids and leafhoppers, due to the resistance of products. Avoid using chemical products during flowering.
- Applications should be carried out early in the pre-flowering stage and, at most, 3 times every 8 days during full flowering.
- To ensure fruits of good size, it is recommended to apply bio-stimulants and multimineral products every 21 days, with a dose of 2 cubic centimeters per liter (cc/lt).
