# Ministry of Public Service

The Ministry of Public Service sets workforce policy for the civil service and
oversees service delivery standards across government departments. It owns the
correspondence-tracking process used between departments and the escalation
path for service-centre cases.

## Responsibilities

- Civil service workforce policies and standards
- Service delivery standards and reporting
- Oversight of the Huduma centre network
  ([Huduma Service Centres](./huduma_kenya.md))

## Relationship to Other Concepts

The ministry maintains the master list of services published in the
[Service Directory](./service_directory.md) and receives escalations that
require a departmental decision.
