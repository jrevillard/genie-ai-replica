## Pests and Diseases

The most common pests affecting corn are: corn earworm, corn borer, aphids, and stalk borer, among others. It is necessary to carry out preventive controls to avoid economic damage to the crop, so it is recommended to carry out periodic inspections and to be able to decide the appropriate time to apply insecticides. Insecticides should be used, preferably those with a green label.

## Weeds

Manual weeding should be carried out within the 10 to 25 days after planting. For chemical control, it is recommended to use products with a green label.

## Harvesting

It should be carried out when the ear of corn reaches its physiological maturity. To achieve better utilization of the nutritional quality of this variety, the grain should not be mixed with other materials, preferably leaving it for family consumption.

## CENTA PORRILLO 2020 BIOFORTIFIED CORN

This is a self-pollinating variety, identified by CENTA in collaboration with the International Maize and Wheat Improvement Center (CIMMYT) and with the financial support of the Harvestplus program. It has a high yield potential compared to self-pollinating varieties. Its main attribute is the higher content of Zinc (Zn) and High Protein Quality (double content of essential amino acid Tryptophan), which is necessary for human nutrition. This new variety is a sustainable alternative to contribute to reducing malnutrition in rural and urban areas, through the consumption of the grain.

According to the International Maize and Wheat Improvement Center (CIMMYT), for a corn variety to be considered of high protein quality, the Protein Index must be superior to 0.80.

## Planting Seasons

First:

May 15th to June 15th.

Second:

August 1st to 31st.

Soil moisture or irrigation: December 1st to January 15th.

## Soil Preparation

Manual or mechanized, in the latter case, a plowing and two harrowing passes (crossing the land) are recommended.

## Before Planting

When planting for the first time, it is recommended to use certified or high-quality seeds for the CENTA Porrillo 2020 variety to ensure the physical and genetic purity of the variety; a minimum content of Tryptophan and Zinc.

Seed treatment is necessary to prevent various soil pests such as wireworms and cutworms, blind beetles, and aphids.

## Planting Densities

The recommended spacing is: 0.75 or 0.80 meters between rows and 0.40 meters between plants, two plants per row, for a density of 43,750 per manzana (a unit of area).

## Fertilization

It is recommended to take soil samples to determine the type and amount of fertilizer needed for the crop. If this is not possible, the following fertilization schedule is suggested:

- First fertilization: at planting or 10 days after seedlings emerge. Apply 4.0 qq/mz of a 15-15-15 or 16-20-0 formula. Apply it in a band for optimal utilization.
- Second fertilization: apply 4.00 qq/mz of 21% Ammonium Sulfate between 25 and 30 days after planting. If mechanized, apply at the time of sowing.
- Third fertilization: apply 1.5 qq/mz of 46% Urea 35 to 40 days after planting.
- Ammonium Sulfate and Urea do not need to be banded; good soil moisture is sufficient for optimal utilization.
