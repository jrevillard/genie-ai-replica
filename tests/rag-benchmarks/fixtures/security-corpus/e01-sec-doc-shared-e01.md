## Ownership

The flood-response plan is owned by the Regional Emergency Management Agency.
The agency maintains the plan, issues its yearly revision, and chairs the
flood-response coordination group that meets before every rainy season.

## Scope of the plan

The plan covers the three river basins of the region and assigns the lead
role for early warning, evacuation routing and post-flood damage assessment.
The Regional Emergency Management Agency operates the warning siren network
and publishes the sandbag depot locations each March; the municipal public
works department executes the physical deployments the plan orders.

Any amendment to the flood-response plan is countersigned by the Regional
Emergency Management Agency before it takes effect, and the version history
of the plan is kept in the agency's document register.
